# Governance

Lictory is maintainer-led. Maintainers decide product direction, accept
contributions, manage releases and appoint additional maintainers. The current
maintainer contact is the repository's owner; the permanent public organization
and legal copyright identity are still being established.

Discuss substantial behavior changes in a proposal issue before implementation.
Record the problem, alternatives, domain invariants, affected delivery modes and
maintainer decision in that discussion. A contribution is not accepted merely
because it passes CI. Review emphasizes authorization, history, human decisions,
clarity and the smallest model that preserves the behavior.

Anyone can contribute code, documentation, bug reports or design feedback under
[the contribution rules](CONTRIBUTING.md). Maintainers may grant responsibility
for a domain after sustained contributions and review. Write access does not
waive review or required verification. Use CODEOWNERS and branch rules to protect
sensitive areas; update owner handles when the repository moves.

Disagreements should be discussed with evidence in the relevant issue or pull
request. The responsible maintainer records the decision and its rationale.
Repeated or sensitive conduct concerns go through the private reporting route in
[the code of conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

There is no foundation, elected council, commercial support promise or feature
roadmap implied by this document. Changes to licensing, contribution terms or
the community/cloud boundary require an explicit public proposal and a rights
review. Existing contributor copyrights do not automatically transfer to the
maintainer.
