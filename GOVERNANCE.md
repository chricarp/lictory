# Governance

Lictory is maintainer-led. Christian Carpinelli ([@chricarp](https://github.com/chricarp))
is the current maintainer and makes final scope, release and access decisions.
Contributors retain credit for their work; contributing does not automatically
grant repository permissions or a commitment to merge.

Use issues for reproducible bugs and scoped proposals. A maintainer's explicit
agreement on a feature's direction and scope is needed before implementation.
Record durable technical decisions in `docs/internals/`, link them from the
issue, and keep security reports private. Disagreement is welcome when it
addresses the behavior, evidence and tradeoffs. A maintainer records the final
decision and reason; new evidence can reopen a decision.

Maintainers triage reports, review changes, enforce the code of conduct and
coordinate releases and security fixes. There is no guaranteed response time,
paid support entitlement or bug bounty. The public roadmap consists of accepted
proposal issues; acceptance is direction, not a delivery date.

Additional maintainers may be invited after sustained contributions and review
work. Grant the least access needed, require two-factor authentication, and
review access when responsibilities change. Before adding maintainers, document
how another maintainer can handle private reports and emergency releases.

Changes to governance, licensing and contribution terms require an explicit
maintainer decision. They cannot retroactively remove rights already granted
under an applicable license. Publication and repository administration checks are described in
[the repository runbook](docs/operations/open-source.md).
