Notifications exist to unblock work. Lictory tells you when something is
asked of you, or when something changed in work you are part of. It does not
tell you about every edit. Notifications appear under **Updates** in the
[Inbox](/inbox), and the important ones are also emailed.

## What notifies you by default

The defaults follow one rule:

- **Asks something of you**: in the Inbox **and by email**. A review is
  requested, you are mentioned, a blocking discussion is opened, your
  approval went stale, you are assigned, an export failed.
- **Informs you**: in the Inbox only. A status changed, someone replied, a
  discussion was resolved, an iteration started.
- **Noisy**: off until you turn it on. Every new revision of a specification.

| Type                           | Notification                                                                                                        | Default         |
| ------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------- |
| **Reviews and approvals**      | **Review requested**                                                                                                | Inbox and email |
|                                | **Review decision** (approved, changes requested, revoked)                                                          | Inbox and email |
|                                | **Your approval went stale**                                                                                        | Inbox and email |
|                                | **Ready for delivery**                                                                                              | Inbox and email |
|                                | **Review cancelled**, **No longer ready**                                                                           | Inbox           |
|                                | **Specification revised**                                                                                           | Off             |
| **Discussions and mentions**   | **Mentioned**, **Blocking discussion**                                                                              | Inbox and email |
|                                | **New discussion**, **Reply**, **Discussion resolved or reopened**                                                  | Inbox           |
| **Branches and pull requests** | **Pull request review requested**, **Pull request decision**, **Pull request ready to merge**                       | Inbox and email |
|                                | **Pull request merged**                                                                                             | Inbox           |
|                                | **Suggestion review requested**, **Suggestion decision**                                                            | Inbox and email |
|                                | **Suggestion updated**, **Suggestion merged or closed**                                                             | Inbox           |
| **Work items**                 | **Assigned to you**                                                                                                 | Inbox and email |
|                                | **Status changed**, **Archived or restored**, **Blocked by another item**                                           | Inbox           |
| **Iterations**                 | **Iteration started**, **Iteration completed**                                                                      | Inbox           |
| **Exports**                    | **Export failed**                                                                                                   | Inbox and email |
|                                | **Export completed**                                                                                                | Inbox           |
| **Pages**                      | **Page review requested**, **Your page approval went stale**, **Mentioned on a page**, **Blocking page discussion** | Inbox and email |
|                                | **Page discussion reply**                                                                                           | Inbox           |

Notices about your organization being scheduled for deletion, or that
deletion being cancelled, always reach every member in the Inbox and by
email. They cannot be turned off.

### A few that matter most

- **Your approval went stale.** The specification changed after you
  approved it, so your approval no longer covers the current revision. Review
  it again ([approvals](/review/approvals)).
- **Export failed.** An export to Jira or another delivery tool finished with
  failures or conflicts. The export shows which items were not published and
  why; fix the cause and retry them ([Jira](/integrations/jira)).
- **Pull request ready to merge.** Every merge requirement is met. The author
  and the branch's collaborators are told once each time it becomes ready. A
  project can turn this off with **Ready to merge notice** in **Project
  settings → Delivery**.

## Who is notified

You receive updates about a work item when you are **involved** in it:

- you created it or are its assignee;
- you requested its review or were named as a reviewer;
- you started or replied to one of its discussions;
- you authored or reviewed one of its suggestions;
- you watch it.

A review requested from a discipline reaches every member of that
discipline. Iteration updates reach the project's members.

Some things are always true:

- You are never notified of your own action.
- One change sends you at most one notification per item: the most specific
  one. A mention beats "new discussion"; a stale approval beats "revised".
- Only current members of the organization are notified.

### Pages

Page notifications reach only people who can read the space, checked when
they are sent and again when you read or are emailed them:

- **Mentioned on a page**: you are mentioned in a page discussion;
- **Page discussion reply**: someone replied in a page discussion you took
  part in;
- **Blocking page discussion**: a blocking discussion was opened on a page
  you wrote or contributed to;
- **Page review requested** and **Your page approval went stale**: you are
  asked to review a page change, or one you approved changed.

Opening or resolving a non-blocking discussion on a page notifies nobody
([comments](/spaces/comments)).

## Mention someone

Type `@` in a discussion and choose a person. They are notified with a link
to the discussion. Mentioning someone who is not a member, or who cannot read
the page's space, notifies nobody.

## Watch an item

Choose **Watch** in a work item's header (under **More actions** on a
phone) to get its updates without being involved. The button then shows
**Watching**; choose it again to stop.

Items you are involved in show **Watching** already. Choosing it stops their
ambient updates (status changes, replies, new discussions). Requests
addressed to you still reach you: review requests, mentions, assignment and
stale approvals follow your notification settings only.

Pages cannot be watched; their notifications follow involvement only.

## Choose what notifies you

Open **Notification settings** from the Inbox
(`/your-org/inbox/settings`).

![Notification settings for the Northwind organization, grouped by type, each notification with an on/off switch and "Also send an email"](/screenshots/inbox-settings.webp)

<Steps>
<Step>

### Choose where the settings apply

Under **Settings for**, choose the organization (**Everywhere**), a project
or a team. A team's settings win for its items, then the project's, then the
organization's, then the defaults. Items without a team use the project's
settings; pages use the settings of the space's team or project.

</Step>
<Step>

### Turn notifications on or off

Each notification has a switch for the Inbox. When it is on, tick **Also
send an email** to get it by email too. Email is always a copy of an Inbox
notification, never sent on its own.

Each row says where its value comes from: **Default** or **From organization
settings**. A value you set has **Reset**.

</Step>
<Step>

### Change many at once

**No email** turns email off for every notification in this scope. **Turn
all off** turns everything off. **Reset to defaults** (organization) or
**Use organization settings** (project or team) removes your choices for
this scope.

</Step>
</Steps>

Settings are personal: they change only what you receive.

## Email

Email goes out within moments of the notification, and is retried if the
mail service fails. It is not sent when:

- your installation has no email configured
  ([email settings](/self-hosting/configuration#email));
- you archived the notification first, or left the organization;
- you can no longer read the space a page notification is about.

## What to watch for

- **No digest.** Lictory sends each email on its own rather than a daily
  summary. Keep email for what asks something of you.
- **Muting is per item.** Unwatching one item does not change your settings
  for its project.
- **Turning off review requests hides real work.** The **To do** view still
  shows them, but you will not be told.

## Tips

- Keep the email defaults: they cover only what blocks someone.
- Turn on **Specification revised** for a project only while you are its
  main reviewer, then turn it off again.
- Watch the few items you depend on but do not work on, instead of turning
  on notifications for the whole project.
