---
title: Audit log
description: See who changed membership, roles, access tokens and configuration, and what agents did on someone's behalf; filter it and export it.
---

The audit log answers "who changed this, when, and to whom" for the parts of
an organization that decide who can do what: who belongs, who administers,
which tokens can act in it, how it is configured, and what agents did for
people. Only organization administrators can read it.

It is separate from an item's activity. Revisions, reviews, approvals,
discussions and merges are recorded on each work item and page, where
everyone who can read the item sees them.

## What is recorded

| Activity            | Entries                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| ------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Members & roles** | Members added, joined (accepted an invitation), removed, left or deleted their account; application role changes; invitations sent, withdrawn or declined                                                                               |
| **Access tokens**   | Agent tokens created (with access level and expiry) and revoked, in every organization the token's owner belongs to; two-factor requirement changes and resets                                                                          |
| **Organization**    | The organization created, renamed or given a new handle; data exports requested and downloaded; the audit log exported; deletion scheduled or cancelled                                                                                 |
| **Configuration**   | Every configuration change, with the previous definition: project settings, members and project roles, disciplines, teams, workflow, types, labels and milestones, readiness, spaces and their access, integrations (never credentials) |
| **Agent calls**     | Every call an agent made through MCP and every action Litto took: the person, the token's name, the operation and its outcome                                                                                                           |

Each entry names who acted (or **Lictory** when the system did), with their
name and email at the time, what they acted on, and when. Passwords, token
values, credentials and specification content are never recorded.

Not recorded here: sign-ins and changes people make to their own account
(password, email, sessions), which belong to the person rather than an
organization.

## Find something

1. Go to **Organization settings → Audit log**.
2. Narrow it with **Activity** (**All activity**, **Members & roles**,
   **Access tokens**, **Organization**, **Configuration**, **Agent calls**),
   **Person** (or **Anyone**), and **From** and **To** dates (UTC days).
3. **Show older entries** loads more.

## Export it

**Export CSV** downloads the entries matching the current filters as a
spreadsheet. The export itself is recorded in the log, with its filters.

## What to watch for

- **Entries cannot be edited or deleted**, not even by administrators. They
  are kept for the life of the organization; there is no automatic expiry.
- **Deleting the organization deletes its audit log.** The installation's
  own history keeps a record that the organization was deleted, by whom and
  what was removed ([instance administration](/organization/instance#history)).
- **A deleted account's entries stay**, with the name and email recorded at
  the time; that person's agent call records are removed.
- **Agents cannot read the log.** It is reserved for administrators in the
  browser.

## Tips

- Filter on **Agent calls** and a person to review what an agent did before
  you trust it with a read/write token for longer.
- Export the log before you schedule an organization's deletion if your
  company must keep it; the [data export](/organization/data) includes it
  too.
- Name agent tokens after their job ("Claude spec reviewer"): the log shows
  the token's name on every call.
