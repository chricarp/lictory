People join an organization by accepting an administrator's invitation.
Projects and disciplines are set in each project.

![Organization settings, Members: the invitation form with a project and disciplines, and the member list with roles and projects](/screenshots/org-members.webp)

## Invite someone

<Steps>
<Step>

### Open Members

Go to **Organization settings → Members** (organization administrators
only).

</Step>
<Step>

### Enter the person

Under **Invite to the organization**, type their email address, or the
`@username` of someone who already has an account on your installation.

</Step>
<Step>

### Choose a project and disciplines (optional)

Pick a **Project** (or keep **Organization only**) and tick their
disciplines, such as Engineering. They apply on acceptance.

</Step>
<Step>

### Send it

**Send invitation** emails a link, which the page also shows for sharing
another way. It is accepted by signing in with the invited address.

</Step>
</Steps>

People without an account create one from the invitation, as your
installation's [access policy](/organization/instance#access-policy) allows
(by default, a new account needs a pending invitation). People with one see
the invitation when they sign in.

### Pending invitations

**Withdraw** cancels a pending invitation; its link stops working.

## Accept an invitation

Open the link, sign in with the invited address (or create your account),
then **Accept invitation**. You land in the organization, and in any project
and disciplines the invitation named. If one of those is no longer available
you still join, and Lictory says what could not be added so an
administrator can add it.

## Roles

- **Make admin** lets a member manage the organization and every project.
  **Make member** takes it back. The last administrator cannot be demoted.
- **Owners** are administrators who can also delete the organization and
  reset other owners' two-factor. Whoever creates an organization is its
  owner; an instance administrator can also add someone as an owner from the
  [admin console](/organization/instance).

Administrators get no work item capabilities from the role; those come from
[disciplines](/organization/disciplines).

## Add people to a project

In **Project settings → Members**, **Add people** adds organization
members. Each row sets a project role (admin or member) and disciplines.

- Organization administrators manage every project without being listed;
  when listed, they are marked **Organization admin**.
- Adding someone to a discipline or a team also makes them a project member.
- Removing someone from the project removes their disciplines and teams in
  it.

## What to watch for

- **Removing someone from the organization** is not available in
  **Organization settings**. An instance administrator can deactivate their
  account (reversible) in the [admin console](/organization/instance#people),
  and people can [delete their own account](/basics/account#delete-your-account).
  Removing them from projects and disciplines takes away capabilities there,
  not organization membership.
- **Changing an owner** has no control in Organization settings yet. Decide
  the owner when creating the organization.
- **Invitations by username** reach only people who already have an account.
  With the installation's sign-up mode set to `closed`, invitations by email
  reach existing accounts only too.
- People who leave keep their name on their contributions, as a former
  member.

## Tips

- Invite with the project and disciplines filled in, so a new reviewer can
  be asked to review on day one.
- Keep two administrators, so access never depends on one person.
- Use disciplines, not the administrator role, to let someone review or
  edit specifications.
