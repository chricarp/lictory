---
title: Using Lictory on a phone
description: What moves where on a phone, and which work is easier on a larger screen.
---

Lictory runs in your phone's browser; there is no app to install. Open your
installation's address and sign in. Everything you can do on a laptop is
there, arranged in one column.

A phone keeps review moving between meetings: read a specification, answer
a discussion, approve a revision, check the board, clear your Inbox. Write
long specifications and reorganize work on a larger screen.

## The frame

The organization rail and the sidebar become a drawer. The top bar has
**Open navigation** (the menu button, with a dot when something waits on
you), the current page, project or space, **Search**, **Create** (in a
project where you can create items) and **Ask Litto**.

![The Inbox on a phone: the top bar with the menu, Search, Create and Litto, the Updates and To do views with filters, and recent notifications](/screenshots/phone-inbox.webp)

## A work item

The item header keeps the way back, the key, **Knowledge**, **Discussions**
and **Review & details** (the panel icon). The rest moves into **More
actions** (**⋯**): the parent item, **Compare revisions**, **Watch**, **Copy
link**, export to Jira, and **Archive**.

- Priority, and in projects delivered in Lictory status and owner, sit
  under the title, editable without opening the details.
- **Review & details** opens the review rail as a drawer: every property,
  readiness checks and reviews.
- **Discussions** opens as a bottom sheet. `Esc` (or the close button) puts
  you back where you were in the text.
- **Knowledge** scrolls to the pages about the item under the
  specification.

## Board, backlog and previews

The board scrolls sideways, one column at a time. A card's preview opens as
a bottom sheet; **Open** goes to the full item. Backlog rows show key, title,
refinement state and estimate.

![The board on a phone: the Backlog column and the Todo column with PAY-3, PAY-4 and PAY-7, scrolling sideways to the other columns](/screenshots/phone-board.webp)

## Spaces

A space's page tree is its home page, with each page's actions behind
**⋯**. A page puts its primary action and discussions in one row under the
title, and the version line is shortened (_Published v2_; tap it for
details). The editor's toolbar is one row that scrolls sideways. See
[pages](/spaces/pages#on-a-phone).

![The Payments service page on a phone: Published v1, 1 discussion, Edit, the text with item chips, and a decision card](/screenshots/phone-page-reader.webp)

## Litto

Litto opens as a sheet over the page. Text you selected before opening it
travels with your question.

## Easier on a larger screen

- **Side by side.** The list beside an open item appears from about 1040
  pixels of width, and the review rail beside the text from about 850. On a
  phone each is a separate screen or a drawer.
- **Dragging.** Moving cards across the board and ranking the backlog use
  drag and drop, which many phone browsers do not offer. Change the status,
  sprint or cycle in the item's properties instead.
- **Comparing revisions.** Word-level diffs of long specifications read
  best wide.
- **Writing.** Long specifications, tables and the breakdown into child
  items are quicker with a keyboard ([shortcuts](/basics/shortcuts)).
- **Settings.** Organization and project settings work on a phone but are
  laid out for a larger screen.

## Tips

- Add your installation to your home screen from the browser's share menu
  to open it like an app.
- Turn on the notifications that unblock you (review requested, mentions)
  and read the rest as a digest: see
  [notification settings](/inbox/notifications).
- When a discussion needs more than a few lines, reply "I'll take this at my
  desk" and leave it open rather than resolving it from the phone.
