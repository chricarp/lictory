---
title: Using Lictory on a phone
description: What Lictory looks like on a phone, what moves where, and which work is easier on a larger screen.
---

Lictory runs in your phone's browser; there is no app to install. Open your
installation's address and sign in as usual. The layout adapts to the
width of the screen, so everything you can do on a laptop is there, arranged
for one column.

Use a phone to keep review moving between meetings: read a specification,
answer a discussion, approve a revision, check the board, clear your Inbox.
Write long specifications and reorganize work on a larger screen.

## The frame

The sidebar becomes a drawer. The bar at the top has **Open navigation**
(the menu button), the name of the project or space you are in, **Search**,
**Create** (in a project where you can create items) and **Ask Litto**.

![The Inbox on a phone: the top bar with the menu, Search, Create and Litto, the Updates and To do views with filters, and recent notifications](/screenshots/phone-inbox.webp)

## A work item

The item header keeps what a reviewer reaches for: the way back, the item's
key, **Knowledge**, **Discussions** and **Review & details** (the panel
icon). The rest moves into **More actions** (**⋯**): the parent item,
**Compare revisions**, **Watch**, **Copy link**, export to Jira, and
**Archive**.

- Priority, and in projects delivered in Lictory status and owner, sit
  under the title, so you can change them without opening the details.
- **Review & details** opens the review rail as a drawer: every property,
  readiness checks and reviews.
- **Discussions** opens as a sheet from the bottom of the screen. `Esc` (or
  the close button) puts you back where you were in the text.
- **Knowledge** brings the pages about the item into view under the
  specification.

## Board, backlog and previews

The board scrolls sideways, one column after another. Selecting a card
opens its preview as a sheet from the bottom of the screen; **Open** goes to
the full item. The backlog keeps each row short: key, title, refinement
state and estimate.

![The board on a phone: the Backlog column and the Todo column with PAY-3, PAY-4 and PAY-7, scrolling sideways to the other columns](/screenshots/phone-board.webp)

## Spaces

On a narrow screen a space's page tree is its home page, with each page's
actions behind its **⋯** button. A page puts its primary action and its
discussions in one row under the title, and the version line is shortened
(_Published v2_; tap it for the details). The editor's toolbar is one row
that scrolls sideways. See [pages](/spaces/pages#on-a-phone).

![The Payments service page on a phone: Published v1, 1 discussion, Edit, the text with item chips, and a decision card](/screenshots/phone-page-reader.webp)

## Litto

Litto opens as a sheet over the page instead of beside it. Text you
selected before opening it travels with your question.

## Easier on a larger screen

Some things depend on room or on a pointer:

- **Side by side.** The list beside an open item appears from about 1040
  pixels of width, and the review rail beside the text from about 850. On a
  phone each is a separate screen or a drawer.
- **Dragging.** Moving cards across the board and ranking the backlog use
  your browser's drag and drop, which many phone browsers do not offer.
  Change the status, sprint or cycle in the item's properties instead.
- **Comparing revisions.** Word-level diffs of long specifications read
  best wide.
- **Writing.** Long specifications, tables and the breakdown into child
  items are quicker with a keyboard ([shortcuts](/basics/shortcuts)).
- **Settings.** Organization and project settings work on a phone but are
  laid out for a larger screen.

## Tips

- Add your installation to your phone's home screen from the browser's
  share menu, so it opens like an app.
- Turn on the notifications that unblock you (review requested, mentions)
  and read the rest as a digest: see
  [notification settings](/inbox/notifications).
- When a discussion needs more than a few lines, reply "I'll take this at my
  desk" and leave it open rather than resolving it from the phone.
