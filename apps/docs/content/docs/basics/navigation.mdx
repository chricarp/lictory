Lictory has one window: the organization rail and the sidebar on the left,
the work beside them. This page names the parts the rest of the guide refers
to.

## Home

Opening an organization lands on **Home**: what needs you, and what is
moving.

- **Waiting on you**: the first few of everything that waits on you:
  questions and approvals from agents, reviews requested of you, pull
  requests and page changes waiting for your approval or a merge, and your
  items with changes requested or blocking discussions. The Inbox's **To
  do** is the whole list ([Inbox](/inbox)).
- **In flight**: work assigned to you that has started.
- **Ready to start**: work that can start now and is yours or nobody's,
  by the rule agents claim by: scope accepted, nothing blocking it. A ready
  specification nobody accepted yet waits on its owner under **Waiting on
  you** instead. In a project delivered in Jira, ready specifications not
  handed off yet are listed here.
- **Recent outcomes**: the last seven days: your delivered work, and agent
  sessions you started (or whose agent you own) that finished or failed. A
  session that delivered a listed item is that item's row. Open a failed one
  to retry it.
- **Recently edited pages** in your [spaces](/spaces).
- **Agents at work**, once your organization has agents: sessions running
  now ([the Factory](/factory)), then those waiting on a teammate (the
  heading then reads **Agents waiting on teammates**).

Administrators of a new organization also see its setup checklist. A member
in no project yet is told who adds them; a project member without a
discipline is told that reviews can't be asked of them until an
administrator gives them one.

Home updates live; there is nothing to refresh.

## The organization rail

On wide screens, a strip on the far left shows every organization you
belong to. Select one to switch; a dot means unread notifications. **+**
creates another organization, where your installation allows it. On smaller
screens the organization menu at the top of the sidebar does the same; on a
phone the strip is in the navigation drawer.

## The sidebar

From top to bottom:

- **The organization menu** (your organization's name or logo): switch
  organizations, **Organization settings**, **Create organization**.
- **Collapse app sidebar** (`[`) folds the sidebar into icons. Drag its edge
  to resize (double-click the edge to reset).
- **Search** (`Ctrl K`, `⌘ K` or `/`) opens the [command palette](#the-command-palette).
- **New item** (the pencil beside Search, or `C`) creates a work item in the
  current project.
- **Home**, then **Inbox**: notifications and to-dos that need you. Its
  badge counts your to-dos (the same number as Home's **Waiting on you**); a
  dot means unread updates. **Litto** opens your conversations with
  Lictory's assistant ([Litto](/ai/litto)).
- **Factory**: tabs for the overview, your **Agents** and their **Runners**.
  A dot means agent sessions wait for your answer or approval; the Inbox
  count already includes them ([the Factory](/factory)). **Code** shows
  repositories and pull requests.
- **Projects**: the current project and its views (below). The project
  switcher chooses another; the gear opens **Project settings**. Outside the
  project's pages (Home, a channel, a space) its views fold under the
  switcher so your conversations stay in view; the arrow beside the name
  unfolds them, remembered in this browser.
- **Spaces**: **All spaces**, the organization's spaces and those of your
  projects and teams. **+** creates a space. See [Spaces](/spaces).
- **Channels**: **All channels** and the ones you joined, bold when unread,
  with a count when you were mentioned. **Direct messages** lists recent
  conversations with people and agents. **+** beside each heading starts a
  channel or conversation. They come last because their lists grow with the
  organization.
- **Organization settings** at the bottom.
- **Your account menu** (your name, at the very bottom): **Account
  settings**, **Privacy preferences**, **Sign out** and the Lictory version.
  Instance administrators also see **Admin console**. The indicator beside
  it is the [sync status](#sync-status-and-offline).

Select a section's heading to fold it (remembered in this browser). A folded
section still shows its count.

### A project's views

Under the project switcher, views are grouped under **Refine** (agreeing on
what to build) and **Deliver** (building it or handing it off):

| Views                                                                                                     | What for                                                                                |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Proposals**, **Refinement**, **My reviews**                                                             | The [refinement](/specifications) queues                                                |
| **Board** and **Backlog** (**Backlog & sprints** or **Backlog & cycles** when the project plans that way) | [Delivery](/delivery)                                                                   |
| **Factory queue**                                                                                         | What agents may claim and why other work waits ([the Factory](/factory/autonomy))       |
| **All items**                                                                                             | Every work item of the project, with filters                                            |
| **Teams**                                                                                                 | One entry per team, yours first: its [backlog](/delivery/teams)                         |
| **Spaces**                                                                                                | The project's own spaces                                                                |
| **Views**                                                                                                 | **Needs review**, **Blocked** and **Recently updated**, folded away until you open them |

In a project that delivers in Jira, **Hand-off** (marked **Jira**) replaces
the board, backlog and Factory queue ([Jira](/integrations/jira)).

## A work item page

Every work item, whatever its type, opens on the same page.

![PAY-2 Retry failed card captures: the header with Knowledge, Discussions and Changes, the specification in the middle, and the review rail with required reviews, readiness checks and properties on the right](/screenshots/specification-review.webp)

### The header

Left to right: the way back to the view you came from (**Refinement**, **My
reviews**, **Proposals** or **All items**), the parent item, the type and key
(select the key to copy it), and who else is looking at the item. On the
right:

- **Knowledge** with a count: the pages about this item. It scrolls to the
  item's [Knowledge section](/knowledge/in-work), or opens it in a popover on
  screens that replace the specification (history, branches, pull requests).
- **Discussions** with the number open (and how many block readiness): the
  [discussion panel](/specifications/discussions) beside the text.
- **Changes** compares revisions ([history](/specifications/history)).
- **Hand off to Jira**, in projects that deliver in Jira, once the item is
  ready.
- **Watch** follows the item ([notifications](/inbox/notifications)).
- **More actions** (**⋯**): **Copy link**, hand off, **Archive** or
  **Restore**.
- **Review & details** opens the review rail when the window is too narrow
  to show it beside the text.
- **Ask Litto** opens the assistant with this item as context, when Lictory
  Intelligence is on.

![The item header: Knowledge 3, 3 discussions with a red marker for a blocking one, and Changes](/screenshots/detail-item-header.webp)

### The document

Under the header, top to bottom:

1. **Changed since you last looked** with **View changes**, when the
   specification changed since your last visit.
2. The title and the **workflow card**: where the item stands, its evidence
   and the next action (start refinement, request a review, accept a
   revision for delivery). When your review is requested, it leads with your
   decision and says whether your earlier approval is outdated.
3. The **specification**, the primary surface ([specifications](/specifications)).
   Types without a specification have a description instead.
4. Its **branches** and **Breakdown** into child items
   ([branches](/specifications/branches), [breakdown](/delivery/breakdown)).
5. **Knowledge** ([pages about this item](/knowledge/in-work)) and
   **Relations** ([relations](/delivery/relations)).
6. **Decisions**, **attachments** and the item's **activity**.

### The review rail

On wide screens the rail beside the document holds **Properties** (status,
refinement, type, team, sprint or cycle, estimate, priority, owner, parent,
milestone, labels), **Readiness** (checks and their evidence), **Review**
(who is asked, their decisions, **Request review**) and **Revision**. Drag
its edge to resize. On narrower windows it is a drawer that **Review &
details** opens. See [reviews](/review/requests) and
[readiness](/review/readiness).

## Previews and split view

- **Preview.** On the board, the backlog and a team's backlog, selecting an
  item opens a preview docked in the corner instead of leaving the list.
  **Open** goes to the full page; `Esc` or a click elsewhere closes it.
  `Ctrl`- or `⌘`-click (or middle-click) opens the full page in a new tab.
- **Split view.** An item opened from **All items**, a refinement queue or a
  saved view keeps that list beside it on wide screens. **Hide work item
  list** (top left of the item) folds it away; drag the divider to resize.
  The list also steps aside when a specification's discussions need the
  room.

## Item addresses

Every item has one address, whatever its type:

```text
https://lictory.example.com/northwind/pay/items/PAY-142
```

- `/northwind/items/PAY-142` (without the project) finds the item in the
  right project, when you only know the key.
- `?from=refinement` (or `proposals`, `reviews`) remembers the queue the item
  was opened from, so the sidebar, the way back and the list beside the item
  stay there. Shared links work without it.
- On the board and the backlog, `?item=PAY-142` is the open preview.
- Filters, sorting and grouping live in the address, so a shared link shows
  the same list.

An item's key never changes, even when it moves to another team, and stays
valid when the item is archived, so links in specifications and pages keep
working.

## The command palette

`Ctrl K` (`⌘ K` on a Mac) or `/` opens the command palette anywhere. Type to
find work items (by title or key, such as `PAY-142`), pages, spaces,
projects and their views, pull requests of connected code hosts, agent
sessions and agents, and commands: **New Story proposal** (one per type that
carries a specification), **Create Task** (and the other delivery types),
**New page in …**, **Start agent session**, **Add runner**, **New agent**,
create an item or a space, **Go to** any part of the sidebar, settings,
switch organization, switch to light or dark appearance, ask Litto, and,
with an item open, **Assign PAY-142 to me** or **Set status: …**. See
[search](/search).

## Sync status and offline

Lictory saves as you work; properties, comments and ordering have no save
button. The indicator beside your name in the sidebar says where your
changes are. While offline, waiting for your help or signed out, it adds a
word (**Offline**, **Not saved**, **Signed out**); while reconnecting its
icon turns. On a phone the same problems show as a strip across the top of
the page; tap it for details. Screen readers announce each change.

| Indicator                        | Meaning                                                                                                    |
| -------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Everything is saved**          | Your workspace is up to date; changes are shared automatically                                             |
| **Saving changes…**              | Changes are on their way to the server                                                                     |
| **Reconnecting…**                | The connection dropped; changes you make now are sent when it is back                                      |
| **You’re offline**               | No connection. Lictory reconnects by itself                                                                |
| **Sync needs your help**         | Some changes were not applied. Their text is kept on this device: open the indicator to copy or discard it |
| **Workspace access interrupted** | Your session expired or your access changed. **Sign in again** to keep saving                              |

When your session expires, a notice asks you to **Sign in again** and brings
you back to the same page. If you lose access to an open organization,
Lictory returns you to your list of organizations.

### What works offline

While a tab stays open, what it loaded stays readable without a connection.
Short interruptions are bridged: changes are kept and sent in order when the
connection is back (also after a reload). After about ten minutes without
the server the workspace becomes read-only until it reconnects, so nothing
is silently lost. Text you were writing (a comment, a title, a
specification) is kept on this device until the server has it.

Lictory cannot be opened from scratch offline, and attachments are not
available offline. Live editing of a specification draft or a page shows
**Offline — changes kept on this device** and resends your edits when you
reconnect.

## Tips

- Jump between projects with the palette: `Ctrl K`, type the project or
  view, `Enter`.
- Share item links without `?from=`: the address is the same for everyone,
  and each reader keeps their own way back.
- In a busy review week, keep **Recently updated** open in a second tab: it
  shows what changed without notifications.
- On a small laptop, fold the sidebar (**Collapse app sidebar**) before the
  review rail: the rail holds the review state you need.
