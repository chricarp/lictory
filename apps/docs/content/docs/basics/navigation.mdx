Lictory has one window for everything: the sidebar on the left, and the work
in a frame beside it. This page names the parts so the rest of the guide can
refer to them.

## The sidebar

From top to bottom:

- **The organization switcher** shows your organization's name or logo.
  Open it to switch to another organization you belong to, to reach
  **Organization settings**, or, where your installation allows it, to
  **Create organization**.
- **Collapse app sidebar** (beside the switcher) folds the sidebar into a
  narrow strip of icons. **Search** (`/`) opens the command palette.
- **New item** (`C`) creates a work item in the project you are in.
- **Inbox** collects the notifications and to-dos that need you. Its badge
  counts unread notifications, or, when everything is read, your open
  to-dos. See [Inbox](/inbox).
- **Litto** opens the conversations with Lictory's assistant. See
  [Litto](/ai/litto).
- **Spaces** lists **All spaces** and the organization's spaces and those
  of your projects and teams. The **+** beside the heading creates a space.
  See [Spaces](/spaces).
- **The project switcher** shows the current project; open it to choose
  another. The gear beside it opens **Project settings**.
- **The project's views** follow the work (below).
- **Organization settings** sits at the bottom.
- **Your account menu** (your name at the very bottom) has **Account
  settings**, **Privacy preferences**, **Sign out** and the version of
  Lictory you are using. Instance administrators also see **Admin console**.
  The small indicator beside it is the [sync status](#sync-status-and-offline).

### A project's views

Inside a project the sidebar groups the views by what you do with the work:

| Group        | Views                                                                                                                               |
| ------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Prepare**  | **Proposals**, **Refinement**, **Reviews**: the [refinement](/specifications) queues                                                |
| **Delivery** | **Board** and **Backlog** (**Backlog & sprints** or **Backlog & cycles** when the project plans that way). See [Deliver](/delivery) |
| **Browse**   | **All items**, every work item of the project with filters                                                                          |
| **Teams**    | One entry per team, yours first: the [team's backlog](/delivery/teams)                                                              |
| **Spaces**   | The project's own spaces                                                                                                            |
| **Views**    | **Needs review**, **Blocked** and **Recently updated**. Fold the group away with its heading                                        |

In a project that delivers in Jira, the Delivery group is called
**Delivery · Jira** and holds **Hand-off** instead of the board and backlog
([Jira](/integrations/jira)).

## A work item page

Every work item, whatever its type, opens on the same page.

![PAY-2 Retry failed card captures: the header with Knowledge, Discussions and Changes, the specification in the middle, and the review rail with required reviews, readiness checks and properties on the right](/screenshots/specification-review.webp)

### The header

From left to right: the way back to the view you came from (**Refinement**,
**Reviews**, **Proposals** or **All items**), the parent item, the item's
type and key (select the key to copy it), and who else is looking at the
item right now. On the right:

- **Knowledge** with a count: the pages about this item. It brings the
  item's [Knowledge section](/knowledge/in-work) into view, or, on screens
  that replace the specification (history, branches, pull requests), opens
  it in a popover.
- **Discussions** with the number of open discussions (and how many block
  readiness). It opens the [discussion panel](/specifications/discussions)
  beside the text.
- **Changes** compares revisions ([history](/specifications/history)).
- **Export to Jira**, in projects that deliver in Jira, once the item is
  ready.
- **Watch** to follow the item ([notifications](/inbox/notifications)).
- **More actions** (**⋯**): **Copy link**, export, and **Archive** or
  **Restore**.
- **Review & details** opens the review rail when the window is too narrow
  to show it beside the text.
- **Ask Litto** opens the assistant with this item as context, when
  Lictory Intelligence is on.

![The item header: Knowledge 3, 3 discussions with a red marker for a blocking one, and Changes](/screenshots/detail-item-header.webp)

### The document

Under the header, top to bottom:

1. A note **Changed since you last looked** with **View changes**, when the
   specification changed since your last visit.
2. The title and the **workflow card**: one sentence saying where the item
   stands, its evidence, and the next action (start refinement, request a
   review, accept a revision for delivery).
3. The **specification**, the primary surface ([specifications](/specifications)).
   Items of a type without a specification have a description instead.
4. Its **branches** and **Breakdown** into child items
   ([branches](/specifications/branches), [breakdown](/delivery/breakdown)).
5. **Knowledge** ([pages about this item](/knowledge/in-work)) and
   **Relations** ([relations](/delivery/relations)).
6. **Decisions**, **attachments** and the item's **activity**.

### The review rail

On wide screens a rail sits to the right of the document: the item's
**Properties** (status, refinement, type, team, sprint or cycle, estimate,
priority, owner, parent, milestone, labels), **Readiness** (the checks and
their evidence), **Review** (who is asked to review, their decisions,
**Request review**) and **Revision**. Drag its edge to make it wider or narrower. On
narrower windows it folds into a drawer that **Review & details** opens. See
[reviews](/review/requests) and [readiness](/review/readiness).

## Previews and split view

- **Preview.** On the board, the backlog and a team's backlog, selecting an
  item opens a preview docked in the corner of the screen instead of leaving
  the list, so you can read an item and move on to the next. **Open** goes to the full page;
  `Esc` or a click elsewhere closes the preview. Clicking with `Ctrl` or `⌘`
  (or the middle button) opens the full page in a new tab instead.
- **Split view.** An item opened from **All items**, a refinement queue or a
  saved view keeps that list beside it on wide screens. **Hide work item
  list** (top left of the item) folds the list away; drag the divider to
  resize it. The list also steps aside on its own when a specification's
  discussions need the room.

## Item addresses

Every item has one address, whatever its type:

```text
https://lictory.example.com/northwind/pay/items/PAY-142
```

- `/northwind/items/PAY-142` (without the project) finds the item in the
  right project; use it when you only know the key.
- `?from=refinement` (or `proposals`, `reviews`) remembers the queue the item
  was opened from, so the sidebar, the way back and the list beside the item
  stay there. Links you share work without it.
- On the board and the backlog, `?item=PAY-142` is the open preview.
- Filters, sorting and grouping of lists live in the address too, so a
  shared link shows the same list.

An item's key never changes, even when the item moves to another team. Keys
stay valid when an item is archived, so links in specifications and pages
keep working.

## The command palette

`Ctrl K` (`⌘ K` on a Mac) or `/` opens the command palette anywhere. Type to
find work items (by title or key, such as `PAY-142`), pages, spaces,
projects and their views, and commands: create an item or a space, go to the
Inbox or Organization settings, ask Litto, and, while an item is open,
**Assign PAY-142 to me** or **Set status: …**. See [search](/search).

## Sync status and offline

Lictory saves as you work; there is no save button for properties,
comments or ordering. The indicator beside your name in the sidebar says
where your changes are:

| Indicator                        | Meaning                                                                                                    |
| -------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Everything is saved**          | Your workspace is up to date; changes are shared automatically                                             |
| **Saving changes…**              | Changes are on their way to the server                                                                     |
| **Reconnecting…**                | The connection dropped; changes you make now are sent when it is back                                      |
| **You’re offline**               | No connection. Lictory reconnects by itself                                                                |
| **Sync needs your help**         | Some changes were not applied. Their text is kept on this device: open the indicator to copy or discard it |
| **Workspace access interrupted** | Your session expired or your access changed. **Sign in again** to keep saving                              |

When your session expires, a notice asks you to **Sign in again**; you come
back to the same page. If you lose access to an organization while it is
open, Lictory returns you to your list of organizations.

### What works offline

While a tab stays open, what it has loaded stays readable without a
connection, and short interruptions are bridged: changes are kept and sent,
in order, when the connection is back (also after a reload).
After about ten minutes without the server the workspace becomes read-only
until it reconnects, so nothing you do is silently lost. Text you were
writing (a comment, a title, a specification you were editing) is kept on
this device until the server has it.

Lictory cannot be opened from scratch without a connection, and attachments
are not available offline. Live editing of a specification draft or a page
shows **Offline — changes kept on this device** and sends your edits again
when you reconnect.

## Tips

- Use the palette rather than the sidebar to jump between projects:
  `Ctrl K`, type the project or the view, `Enter`.
- Share item links without `?from=`: the address is the same for everyone,
  and each reader keeps their own way back.
- Keep **Recently updated** open in a second tab during a busy review
  week; it shows what changed without notifications.
- On a small laptop, fold the sidebar (**Collapse app sidebar**) before
  folding the review rail: the rail holds the review state you need.
