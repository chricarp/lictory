## Editing

### "Live editing unavailable"

The editor could not reach Lictory's editing service when it opened the
draft. Reading the page, its history and its comments keep working; only
editing waits. Reload the page after a moment. If it persists for everyone,
the server's `collaboration` service is down or your network blocks its
WebSocket: ask whoever runs your installation, and see
[running Spaces on your server](#running-spaces-on-your-server).

### Is my text safe if the connection drops?

Yes, while the tab stays open. The editor says **Offline — changes kept on
this device**, keeps your edits in the tab and sends them when the
connection returns. Closing the tab or the browser before then loses what
was never saved, and the tab warns you before it closes. Opening a page or
starting to edit needs a connection: Spaces is not an offline editor. See
[offline, honestly](/spaces/editing#offline-honestly).

### Does "Saved" mean everyone can read it?

No. **Saved** means the server has stored your edits in the live draft.
Readers see them after someone publishes the draft (Direct spaces) or a pull
request with them merges (branches). See [publishing](/spaces/publishing).

### "This page was replaced while you had unsaved changes"

Someone restored a version, synced the branch or merged into it while you
had edits the server had not stored yet. Your text is kept in this browser:
**Copy text**, **Insert at end** of the new draft, or **Discard** it. See
[when the draft is replaced](/spaces/editing#when-the-draft-is-replaced).

### Why can't I add images?

Images are stored as private page files, which needs file storage on your
installation. Without it the editor says _Images are unavailable_; your
administrator can set it up ([file storage](/self-hosting/configuration#file-storage)).

## Publishing and reviews

### Why can't I publish in this space?

One of these:

- **The space is Reviewed.** Its pages have no live draft to publish: choose
  **Start a branch**, then propose your change in a pull request
  ([Reviewed spaces](/spaces/publishing#reviewed-spaces)).
- **You can view or comment, but not edit.** Ask someone who manages the
  space for edit access, or join the project or team it belongs to
  ([access](/spaces/access)).
- **The space or page is archived.** Archived content is read-only until it
  is restored.
- **Nothing changed.** _No changes to publish_ means the draft equals the
  published version.

Open blocking comments never stop **Publish** in a Direct space.

### Why did my approval go stale?

Approvals belong to the exact result of the merge you inspected. A new
commit on the branch, a newly published version on main or a sync changed
that result, so your approval shows **Review outdated** and you were
notified. Look at the new resulting changes and approve again if they are
still right. Changes that leave the result identical keep approvals. See
[approvals belong to the exact result](/spaces/branches#approvals-belong-to-the-exact-result).

### Why can't I approve this pull request?

You contributed to it: you authored or typed in one of the commits it would
merge. Nobody approves their own changes, and that includes changes their
agent proposed. If you work alone, use a Direct space.

### Why is "Merge pull request" disabled?

The pull request's **Merge checks** list each reason in plain words:
uncommitted live changes, conflicts, blocking discussions, missing or
pending required reviews, or a request for changes. See
[merge](/spaces/branches#merge).

### My branch has conflicts. Where do I resolve them?

On the branch: **Resolve conflicts** on the pull request (or **Sync from
main** in the branch list) shows each conflict with **Base**, **Ours** and
**Theirs** and lets you pick a side, keep both or edit by hand. See
[conflicts](/spaces/branches#conflicts).

### Can I undo a publish or a merge?

Versions are never deleted or rewritten. Restore the earlier version: that
creates a new version with its content, and the history keeps both. In a
Reviewed space, start a branch from the earlier version and propose it. See
[restore a version](/spaces/history#restore-a-version).

## Access

### Someone can see the space but cannot comment

In an Inherited space whose location is a project or a team, everyone else
in the organization can view but not comment. Add them under **Space
settings → Access → Give access** with **Can comment** or **Can edit**.

### Can I hide one page from part of the team?

Not inside a space: access belongs to the space. Put the page in its own
Restricted space, which can have the same location so it is listed beside
the other one. See [access](/spaces/access#no-page-level-restrictions).

### As an administrator, why can't I open a Restricted space?

Administering a space does not let you read it. On the **Spaces** page,
under **Restricted spaces you administer**, choose **Grant myself access…**.
The grant is recorded in the audit log. See
[administrators](/spaces/access#administrators).

## Pages and work items

### Should this be a page or a work item specification?

Behavior the team is about to build belongs in the work item's
specification: it is refined, reviewed by the project's disciplines, checked
for readiness and delivered. Knowledge that outlives one piece of work (how a
service works, a runbook, a decision record) belongs on a page. Mention the
work item in the page with `#` to connect them.

### Where did my page go?

It may have been moved (its link still works: pages are addressed by id) or
archived. Look in **Show archived pages** in the space's tree, or search its
title with **Find a page** or the command palette.

## Running Spaces on your server

Live editing is served by the `collaboration` service, which ships in the
same image as the API and is part of every installation. Nothing needs to be
configured for it; behind your own reverse proxy, pass WebSocket upgrades
for `/collaboration`. Run exactly one per database: a second copy refuses to
start (`collaboration.owner_busy`).

- What it is and what it needs: [self-hosting overview](/self-hosting) and
  [configuration → live editing](/self-hosting/configuration#live-editing)
  (its memory limit and heap).
- Proxy rules: [reverse proxy and TLS](/self-hosting/reverse-proxy).
- Health checks and alerts: [monitoring](/self-hosting/monitoring).
- Upgrades pause live editing for a few seconds while the service is
  replaced; saved edits are stored and unsaved ones are sent again
  ([upgrades](/self-hosting/upgrades)).
- Backups: pages, their history and even unpublished live drafts are in
  PostgreSQL, so your usual database backup covers Spaces
  ([backups](/self-hosting/backups)).
- When editing fails: [troubleshooting](/self-hosting/troubleshooting#live-editing-unavailable-on-pages-in-spaces).
