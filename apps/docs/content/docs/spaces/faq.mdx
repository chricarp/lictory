## Editing

### "Live editing unavailable"

The editor could not reach the editing service. Reading, history and
comments keep working. Reload after a moment. If it persists for everyone,
the server's `collaboration` service is down or your network blocks its
WebSocket: ask whoever runs your installation, and see
[running Spaces on your server](#running-spaces-on-your-server).

### Is my text safe if the connection drops?

Yes, while the tab stays open (**Offline — changes kept on this device**);
edits are sent on reconnect. Closing the tab or browser first loses unsaved
edits, and the tab warns you. Opening a page or starting to edit needs a
connection. See [offline, honestly](/spaces/editing#offline-honestly).

### Does "Saved" mean everyone can read it?

No. **Saved** means the server stored your edits in the live draft. Readers
see them once the draft is published (Direct spaces) or their pull request
merges. See [publishing](/spaces/publishing).

### "This page was replaced while you had unsaved changes"

Someone restored a version, synced the branch or merged into it before your
edits were stored. Your text is kept in this browser: **Copy text**,
**Insert at end** of the new draft, or **Discard**. See
[when the draft is replaced](/spaces/editing#when-the-draft-is-replaced).

### Why can't I add images?

Images need file storage on your installation. Without it the editor says
_Images are unavailable_; your administrator can set it up
([file storage](/self-hosting/configuration#file-storage)).

## Publishing and reviews

### Why can't I publish in this space?

- **The space is Reviewed.** Choose **Start a branch** and propose your
  change in a pull request
  ([Reviewed spaces](/spaces/publishing#reviewed-spaces)).
- **You can view or comment, but not edit.** Ask for edit access, or join
  the space's project or team ([access](/spaces/access)).
- **The space or page is archived.** It is read-only until restored.
- **Nothing changed.** _No changes to publish_ means the draft equals the
  published version.

Open blocking comments never stop **Publish** in a Direct space.

### Why did my approval go stale?

A new commit on the branch, a new version on main or a sync changed the
merge result you approved, so it shows **Review outdated**. Review the new
resulting changes and approve again if they are right. Changes that leave
the result identical keep approvals. See
[approvals belong to the exact result](/spaces/branches#approvals-belong-to-the-exact-result).

### Why can't I approve this pull request?

You authored or typed in one of its commits, or your agent proposed it. If
you work alone, use a Direct space.

### Why is "Merge pull request" disabled?

Its **Merge checks** list each reason: uncommitted live changes, conflicts,
blocking discussions, missing or pending required reviews, or a request for
changes. See
[merge](/spaces/branches#merge).

### My branch has conflicts. Where do I resolve them?

On the branch: **Resolve conflicts** on the pull request (or **Sync from
main** in the branch list) shows **Base**, **Ours** and **Theirs** for each
conflict; pick a side, keep both or edit by hand. See
[conflicts](/spaces/branches#conflicts).

### The discussions on a branch disappeared after a merge

Deleting a branch, or a merge that retires it, deletes its discussions with
every reply; the confirmation said how many. Discussions on main and other
branches are kept. See [when a branch ends](/spaces/branches#when-a-branch-ends).

### Can I undo a publish or a merge?

Restore the earlier version: it becomes a new version, and the history keeps
both. In a Reviewed space, start a branch from the earlier version and
propose it. See
[restore a version](/spaces/history#restore-a-version).

## Access

### Someone can see the space but cannot comment

In an Inherited project or team space, everyone else can view but not
comment. Add them under **Space settings → Access → Give access** with
**Can comment** or **Can edit**.

### Can I hide one page from part of the team?

Not inside a space. Put the page in its own Restricted space with the same
location. See [access](/spaces/access#no-page-level-restrictions).

### As an administrator, why can't I open a Restricted space?

Administering does not grant reading. On the **Spaces** page, under
**Restricted spaces you administer**, choose **Grant myself access…**
(audited). See
[administrators](/spaces/access#administrators).

## Pages and work items

### Should this be a page or a work item specification?

Behavior about to be built belongs in the work item's specification, which
is reviewed, checked for readiness and delivered. Knowledge that outlives
one piece of work (how a service works, a runbook, a decision record)
belongs on a page. Connect them: `#` in the page, `[[` in the
specification, or **Create work item** from page text. See
[knowledge links](/knowledge).

### I mentioned an item on a page, but the item does not list the page

Items list what a page's **published** version says: a mention in a live
draft or on a branch appears once published or merged into main. The page
must also be readable to you and not archived.
See [references follow published versions](/knowledge#references-follow-published-versions).

### A decision card says "Couldn’t refresh"

The page could not recheck the decision (offline, for example) and shows
what it last read (_Couldn’t refresh · last checked 3m ago_). Choose
**Retry**, or wait for it to retry. **Archived** means the decision was
withdrawn on its work item and kept for history.

### Can a page change a work item?

No. Chips, decision cards and work lists only show what Work holds now.
Open the item to change it. **Create work item** creates new work; it never
changes existing work.

### Where did my page go?

It may have been moved (its link still works) or archived. Check **Show
archived pages** in the tree, or search with **Find a page** or the command
palette.

## Running Spaces on your server

Live editing is served by the `collaboration` service, in the same image as
the API and part of every installation. It needs no configuration; behind
your own reverse proxy, pass WebSocket upgrades for `/collaboration`. Run
exactly one per database: a second copy refuses to start
(`collaboration.owner_busy`).

- What it is and what it needs: [self-hosting overview](/self-hosting) and
  [configuration → live editing](/self-hosting/configuration#live-editing)
  (its memory limit and heap).
- Proxy rules: [reverse proxy and TLS](/self-hosting/reverse-proxy).
- Health checks and alerts: [monitoring](/self-hosting/monitoring).
- Upgrades pause live editing for a few seconds while the service is
  replaced; saved edits are stored and unsaved ones are sent again
  ([upgrades](/self-hosting/upgrades)).
- Backups: pages, history and unpublished live drafts are in PostgreSQL,
  covered by your database backup. Images and files on pages are in your
  attachment bucket: back it up too ([backups](/self-hosting/backups)).
- When editing fails: [troubleshooting](/self-hosting/troubleshooting#live-editing-unavailable-on-pages-in-spaces).
