---

**Lictory Spaces** is where a team keeps what it knows that is not a work
item: handbooks, runbooks, architecture notes, decision records, onboarding
guides. Pages are written together in real time and changed through the same
reviewed history as specifications, so you can always see what a page said,
what changed, who changed it and who approved it.

![The published version 2 of the Payments service page in the Engineering Handbook space, with the space's page tree on the left, a highlighted passage under discussion and two work item mentions](/screenshots/spaces-page-reader.webp)

## Spaces and pages

```text
Organization
└─ Space        a home for knowledge, optionally belonging to a project or a team
   └─ Page      nested pages; each has its own history
      ├─ main         the published version readers see
      └─ branches     proposed changes, each with its own live draft
```

- A **space** belongs to the organization and, optionally, to one project or
  one team. That **location** decides where the space is listed and who can
  edit it by default. See [create and manage spaces](/spaces/create).
- A **page** belongs to one space and can sit under another page of the same
  space. See [pages and the page tree](/spaces/pages).
- Every page has a **published version**, the one readers see, and a
  **history** of every earlier version. See
  [versions and history](/spaces/history).
- People write in a **live draft**: a shared working copy that several
  editors change at once. A live draft is never history. Only what someone
  **publishes** or **commits** becomes a version. See
  [writing together](/spaces/editing).

## Pages and work items

Pages are not work items. They have no key, status, estimate, iteration or
readiness, and a page never becomes delivery work. Specifications stay on
their work items, where they are refined, reviewed and checked for readiness
([specifications](/concepts/specifications)). The two link to each other
instead: type `#` in a page to mention a work item such as `PAY-142`, and
readers see its current status, refinement state and readiness.

|                   | Specification                            | Page                                               |
| ----------------- | ---------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| Lives on          | A work item of a project                 | A space                                            |
| Written by        | One author at a time, in Markdown        | Several editors at the same time, in a rich editor |
| Becomes a version | Every material change creates a revision | Publishing or committing the live draft            |
| Reviewed by       | The project's disciplines (Product, QA…) | Anyone who can comment on the space                |
| Feeds             | Readiness checks and delivery            | Nothing: pages are knowledge, not work             |
| Who can see it    | Every organization member                | Decided per space: Inherited or Restricted         |

## How a page changes

Each space chooses one publishing policy:

- **Direct** (the default): editors change the page's live draft together and
  **Publish** it. Every published version stays in the history.
- **Reviewed**: the published page is protected. Changes are proposed on a
  branch, reviewed in a pull request and published when it merges.

<ReviewedChange />

Both policies keep the same guarantees: versions never change after the
fact, approvals belong to the exact result a reviewer inspected, and nothing
is merged or published that nobody saw. See [publishing](/spaces/publishing)
and [branches and pull requests](/spaces/branches).
