---
title: Pages and the page tree
description: Create pages, nest and reorder them with the mouse or the keyboard, archive and restore them, and find the page you need.
---

A **page** belongs to one space and can nest under another page of it, as
deep as you like. The **page tree** shows the structure beside every page and
on the space's home.

## Create a page

People who can edit the space create pages:

- **New page** on the space's home, or the **+** at the top of the tree,
  adds a page at the end of the space.
- **New subpage**, in a page's actions (the **⋯** button in the page header
  or next to the page in the tree), adds one under it.

Enter a **Title**, pick **Start from** (**Blank** or a
[template](/spaces/templates)) and choose **Create page**.

The page is published at once as its first version, with its title or the
template's structure. In a [Reviewed space](/spaces/publishing#reviewed-spaces)
that first version needs no review; every later change arrives through a
pull request.

## Read a page

![A published page: breadcrumbs, “Published version 1 · by Maria Chen” under the title, the discussions button, More page actions and Edit, and item chips in the text](/screenshots/spaces-page-reader.webp)

A page opens on its **published version**. The line under the title names
it and who published it (_Published version 2 · by Maria Chen · just now_),
and in a Reviewed space who approved it. The header holds:

- the **breadcrumbs**: **Spaces**, the space, then the pages above this one
  (on phones the bar keeps the title, and the buttons below move under it);
- the **discussions** button with the number of open discussions, and a red
  dot when one of them is blocking (see [comments](/spaces/comments));
- **More page actions** (**⋯**): **New subpage**, **Move…**, **Archive**,
  then **Version history**, **Branches** and **Pull requests**, and who
  created the page;
- the primary action: **Edit**, **Edit unpublished draft** when unpublished
  changes are waiting, or **Start a branch** in a Reviewed space (see
  [publishing](/spaces/publishing)).

Work item mentions are chips with the item's key, current title and status;
point at or focus one for its refinement state, readiness, owner and
iteration. Page links show the current title (and the cited version when
pinned), or _Unavailable page_ when you cannot open it. Decision cards and
work lists show what Work holds now. See [blocks and chips](/spaces/blocks).

**Work linked to this page** sits under the page. Select text to
**Comment**, **Create work item** or, with Lictory Intelligence on, **Ask
Litto**. See [work in your pages](/knowledge/in-pages).

**Hide pages** (the panel button in the tree's bar) puts the tree away;
**Show pages** in the page header brings it back, and the browser remembers.
While comments are open on a narrower screen, the tree steps aside.

## Nest and reorder pages

Drag a page and drop it **before**, **after** or **into** another. Without a
pointer, **Move…** asks for the **New parent** (filter by title, or the top
level) and the **Position**, **First** or **Last**. Subpages move with it;
pages move only within their space.

Pages are addressed by id, so moving or renaming a page never breaks a link.
Changing the space's address changes its pages' addresses; page links inside
pages follow.

### With the keyboard

The tree follows the standard tree pattern:

| Key                       | Action                                                           |
| ------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Arrow Up / Arrow Down     | Move between visible pages                                       |
| Home / End                | Move to the first or last visible page                           |
| Arrow Right / Arrow Left  | Expand or collapse a page; move to its first child or its parent |
| Enter                     | Open the page                                                    |
| Alt+Arrow Up / Arrow Down | Move the page before or after its sibling                        |
| Shift+F10                 | Open the page's actions (New subpage, Move…, Archive)            |

## Archive and restore

**Archive** hides a page and its subpages from the tree; the confirmation
offers **Undo**. There is no permanent delete: **Show archived pages** lists
them, and **Restore** puts a page back where it was, with the subpages
archived together with it (subpages archived earlier on their own stay
archived).

An archived page keeps its history and discussions, read-only. It leaves
search, and its links stop showing on work items until restored.

## Find a page

- **Find a page** on the space's home narrows the tree to matching titles,
  each with its path (_Payments service · in Architecture_).
- The **command palette** finds spaces and page titles you can read, and
  page text under **In pages**, opening the page at the matching block.
  **See all results for …** lists every match. See [search](/search).

Search reads only published versions; live drafts, branches and pull
requests are never searched.

## On a phone

On a narrow screen the tree becomes the space's home, with page actions
behind **⋯**; a page puts its primary action and discussions on one row
under the title.
