---
title: Pages and the page tree
description: Create pages, nest and reorder them with the mouse or the keyboard, archive and restore them, and find the page you need.
---

A **page** belongs to one space and can sit under another page of the same
space, as deep as you like. The space's **page tree** shows that structure
beside every page and on the space's home.

## Create a page

People who can edit the space create pages:

- **New page** on the space's home, or the **+** at the top of the tree,
  adds a page at the end of the space.
- **New subpage**, in a page's actions (the **⋯** button in the page header
  or next to the page in the tree), adds one under it.

Give it a title and it opens, ready to write. In a
[Reviewed space](/spaces/publishing#reviewed-spaces) the new page is
published with its title and no content; its first content arrives through a
pull request like every other change.

## Read a page

![The Payments service page in its Engineering Handbook tree: breadcrumbs Spaces › Engineering Handbook › Architecture, "Published version 2 by Maria Chen just now", a 1 discussion button, the More actions button and Edit](/screenshots/spaces-page-reader.webp)

A page opens on its **published version**. The line under the title says
which one and who published it, for example _Published version 2 · by Maria
Chen · just now_; in a Reviewed space it also names who approved it. The
header holds:

- the **breadcrumbs**: the space, then the page's parent. Deeper ancestors
  fold into **…** so the trail stays short;
- the **discussions** button with the number of open discussions, and a red
  dot when one of them is blocking (see [comments](/spaces/comments));
- **More page actions** (**⋯**): **New subpage**, **Move…**, **Archive**,
  then **Version history**, **Branches** and **Pull requests**, and who
  created the page;
- the primary action: **Edit**, **Edit unpublished draft** when unpublished
  changes are waiting, or **Start a branch** in a Reviewed space (see
  [publishing](/spaces/publishing)).

Work item mentions show the item's current status, refinement state and
readiness as _you_ can see it now; an item you cannot see shows only its key.
Links to other pages show the linked page's current title, or _Unavailable
page_ when you cannot open it.

Beside a page, **Hide pages** (the panel button before the breadcrumbs) puts
the tree away for more room, and **Show pages** brings it back; this browser
remembers your choice. While comments are open on a narrower screen, the tree
steps aside on its own.

## Nest and reorder pages

Drag a page in the tree and drop it **before**, **after** or **into**
another page. **Move…** does the same without a pointer: choose the **New
parent** (filter the pages by title, or pick the top level of the space) and
the **Position** among its new siblings, **First** or **Last**. A page moves
with all its subpages, and pages move only within their space.

Pages are addressed by their id, not by their place in the tree or their
title, so moving a page or changing its title never breaks a link to it.
Changing the space's address does change its pages' addresses; page links
inside pages follow it on their own.

### With the keyboard

The page tree follows the standard tree pattern, so every action works
without a mouse:

| Key                       | Action                                                           |
| ------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Arrow Up / Arrow Down     | Move between visible pages                                       |
| Home / End                | Move to the first or last visible page                           |
| Arrow Right / Arrow Left  | Expand or collapse a page; move to its first child or its parent |
| Enter                     | Open the page                                                    |
| Alt+Arrow Up / Arrow Down | Move the page before or after its sibling                        |
| Shift+F10                 | Open the page's actions (New subpage, Move…, Archive)            |

## Archive and restore

**Archive** hides a page and its subpages from the tree. Right after
archiving, the confirmation offers **Undo**. Archiving is always reversible
and there is no permanent delete: **Show archived pages** in the tree lists
them, and **Restore** brings a page back where it was. Restoring a
page also restores the subpages that were archived together with it;
subpages archived earlier, on their own, stay archived.

An archived page keeps its history and discussions, read-only.

## Find a page

- **Find a page** on the space's home narrows the tree to the titles that
  match, wherever they sit, and shows each match with its path (_Payments
  service · in Architecture_).
- The **command palette** finds spaces and page titles across every space
  you can read.

## On a phone

On a narrow screen the tree becomes the space's home, with every page's
actions behind its **⋯** button, and a page puts its primary action and its
discussions on one row under the title.
