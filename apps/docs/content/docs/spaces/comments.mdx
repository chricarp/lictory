Comments in Spaces are discussions about something specific: a passage of
the page, or the page as a whole. They stay attached to that text as the
page changes, so nobody has to ask what a comment was about.

## Start a discussion

- **On text.** Select it and choose **Comment**. In the live editor, use
  **Comment on selection** in the toolbar or press **Mod+Alt+M**.
- **On the whole page.** Choose **Comment on the page** at the foot of the
  Comments panel.

Write in the composer and post with **Mod+Enter**. Tick **Blocking** when the
point must be settled before the page's changes can merge. Mention people
with `@` to notify them.

On the published page, selecting text needs a pointer (or the browser's caret
browsing): keyboard users comment on the whole page there, or on text in the
live editor, where the selection is made with the keyboard.

## The Comments panel

The **discussions** button in the page header shows how many discussions are
open, with a red dot when one is blocking, and opens the **Comments** panel:
beside the page on wide screens (open by default when the page has
comments), as a sheet on narrow ones. Escape closes the sheet.

- Discussions are listed in the order of their text in the page, then the
  ones whose text is gone, then comments on the whole page.
- Commented text is tinted in the page (red for blocking discussions).
  Pointing at a discussion or at its text marks the other; **Show in the
  page** scrolls to it.
- Each discussion offers **Reply**, **Resolve** (or **Reopen**) and **Mark
  blocking** (or **Mark non-blocking**).
- Resolved discussions fold away under **Show N resolved**, and can be
  reopened from there.

A discussion belongs to the line it was written on: the published page and
its live draft share theirs, each branch has its own, and a pull request's
discussions stay on the pull request.

## Comments stay on their text

- **While people type**, a comment on the live draft follows its text as it
  moves, even with several people editing at once. Comments written on the
  draft say _On the unpublished draft_ until it is published.
- **When the draft is published or committed**, the comment is pinned to the
  exact words of that version.
- **In later versions**, a comment finds its text again while the passage
  survives. When the words around it were edited, it says _The quoted text
  changed_ and keeps showing the original quote.
- **When the text is deleted**, the comment shows **Original text removed**
  with the quote and a link to the version it was written on.

A comment is never silently moved to other text.

## Blocking comments

**Blocking** marks something that must be settled first. The page header's
count says what blocking comments hold back:

- **In a Direct space**, open blocking comments keep the page's pull requests
  from merging. They do **not** stop **Publish**: a Direct space trusts its
  editors to publish, and the comment stays visible to everyone until it is
  resolved.
- **In a Reviewed space**, they keep changes to the page from merging until
  they are resolved.

When a blocking comment is added, the people who wrote the page (the authors
and contributors of its versions) are notified.

## Who can do what

| Access          | Comments                                                                                    |
| --------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Can view**    | Read every discussion                                                                       |
| **Can comment** | Also start discussions, reply, resolve, reopen, mark blocking or non-blocking, attach files |
| **Can edit**    | The same as comment                                                                         |

Messages are never edited or deleted, and resolving keeps every message:
the history shows who resolved or reopened a discussion and when it became
blocking. Discussions on an archived page or in an archived space are
read-only. People who cannot read a space cannot tell a discussion exists.

## Notifications

Notifications exist to unblock work, so Spaces sends few of them, and only
to people who can still read the space:

| Notification                 | Who receives it                                       | Default        |
| ---------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- | -------------- |
| You were mentioned           | People mentioned in a message                         | In app + email |
| A reply in a discussion      | Its earlier participants                              | In app         |
| A blocking comment on a page | Authors and contributors of the page's versions       | In app + email |
| Your review was requested    | Reviewers asked to review a pull request              | In app + email |
| Your approval went stale     | Reviewers whose approval no longer matches the result | In app + email |

Opening or resolving a non-blocking discussion notifies nobody, and editing
or publishing a page notifies nobody either. Mentioning someone who cannot
read the space notifies nobody. Notification preferences follow the space's
location: its team, then its project, then the organization.
