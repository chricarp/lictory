A comment is a discussion about a passage of the page, or the page as a
whole. It stays attached to its text as the page changes.

![A page with the Comments panel open beside it: a discussion quotes “02:00 UTC”, which is tinted in the page, with Sam Rivera’s question and Reply, Resolve and Mark blocking](/screenshots/spaces-comments.webp)

## Start a discussion

- **On text.** Select it and choose **Comment**. In the live editor, use
  **Comment on selection** in the toolbar or press **Mod+Alt+M**.
- **On the whole page.** Choose **Comment on the page** at the foot of the
  Comments panel.

Post with **Mod+Enter**. Tick **Blocking** when the point must be settled
before the page's changes can merge. `@` mentions notify people; `#` and
`[[` mention work items and pages.

With the keyboard on the published page, `Tab` to the text, select with the
arrow keys (`Shift` extends, see [keyboard
shortcuts](/basics/shortcuts#reading)) and press **Mod+Alt+M**.

## The Comments panel

The **discussions** button in the page header (open count, red dot when one
is blocking) opens the **Comments** panel: beside the page on wide screens
(open by default when there are comments), a sheet on narrow ones that
Escape closes.

- Discussions follow their text's order in the page, then those whose text
  is gone, then comments on the whole page.
- Commented text is tinted (red for blocking). Pointing at a discussion or
  its text marks the other; **Show in the page** scrolls to it.
- Each discussion offers **Reply**, **Resolve** (or **Reopen**) and **Mark
  blocking** (or **Mark non-blocking**).
- Resolved discussions fold under **Show N resolved**, where they can be
  reopened.

A discussion belongs to the line it was written on: the published page and
its live draft share one, each branch has its own, and a pull request's stay
on the pull request. Deleting or retiring a branch deletes its discussions,
after a confirmation that says how many
([when a branch ends](/spaces/branches#when-a-branch-ends)).

## Comments stay on their text

- **While people type**, a comment on the live draft follows its text, and
  says _On the unpublished draft_ until it is published.
- **On publish or commit**, it is pinned to that version's exact words.
- **In later versions**, it finds its text while the passage survives. When
  the words around it were edited, it says _The quoted text changed_ and
  keeps the original quote.
- **When the text is deleted**, it shows **Original text removed** with the
  quote and a link to its version.

A comment is never silently moved to other text.

## Blocking comments

- **In a Direct space**, open blocking comments keep the page's pull requests
  from merging, but do **not** stop **Publish**; the comment stays visible
  until resolved.
- **In a Reviewed space**, they keep changes from merging until resolved.

A new blocking comment notifies the authors and contributors of the page's
versions.

## Who can do what

| Access          | Comments                                                                                    |
| --------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Can view**    | Read every discussion                                                                       |
| **Can comment** | Also start discussions, reply, resolve, reopen, mark blocking or non-blocking, attach files |
| **Can edit**    | The same as comment                                                                         |

Messages are never edited and resolving keeps them all; the history shows
who resolved or reopened a discussion and when it became blocking.
Discussions are deleted only with their branch, and are read-only on
archived pages and spaces. People who cannot read a space cannot tell a
discussion exists.

## Notifications

Spaces sends few notifications, and only to people who can read the space:

| Notification                 | Who receives it                                       | Default        |
| ---------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- | -------------- |
| You were mentioned           | People mentioned in a message                         | In app + email |
| A reply in a discussion      | Its earlier participants                              | In app         |
| A blocking comment on a page | Authors and contributors of the page's versions       | In app + email |
| Your review was requested    | Reviewers asked to review a pull request              | In app + email |
| Your approval went stale     | Reviewers whose approval no longer matches the result | In app + email |

Opening or resolving a non-blocking discussion, editing, publishing, and
mentioning someone who cannot read the space notify nobody. Preferences
follow the space's location: its team, then its project, then the
organization.
