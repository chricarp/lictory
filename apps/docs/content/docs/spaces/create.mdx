## Create a space

Any organization member can create a space: **New space** on the **Spaces**
page, the **+** next to **Spaces** in the sidebar, or **Create space** in the
command palette.

![The New space dialog: an icon button and a Name field, Customize address, an optional Description, and Access & publishing summarized as "Northwind · everyone can edit · direct publishing" with Configure](/screenshots/spaces-new-space.webp)

<Steps>
<Step>

**Name and icon.** Name the space for what it holds (_Engineering
Handbook_, _Payments_, _Architecture decisions_). The button beside the name
picks an icon.

</Step>
<Step>

**Address.** Derived from the name, for example
`/northwind/spaces/engineering-handbook`. **Customize address** changes it:
lowercase letters, digits and single hyphens. It is part of every page's
address, so copied or bookmarked links work only while it stays the same.
Page links inside pages (`[[`) always follow the current address.

</Step>
<Step>

**Description** (optional). One sentence about what belongs here, shown on
the spaces list and the space's home.

</Step>
<Step>

**Access & publishing.** A one-line summary (_Northwind · everyone can edit ·
direct publishing_); **Configure** changes it:

- **Location**: the organization, one of its projects or one of a project's
  teams. It decides where the space is listed and, while access is
  inherited, who can edit it.
- **Access**: **Inherited** (the location's members can edit; everyone else
  in the organization can view) or **Restricted** (only people you add can
  open it, and you get edit access). See [access](/spaces/access).
- **Publishing**: **Direct** (editors publish changes straight to the page)
  or **Reviewed** (changes are proposed on a branch and published only after
  a review approves them). See [publishing](/spaces/publishing).

</Step>
<Step>

**Create space.** The space opens on its home, ready for a first page
(blank or from a [template](/spaces/templates)), which needs edit access. An
administrator who creates an Inherited space for a team or project they are
not in can view it but not write, until they join or name themselves with
edit access.

</Step>
</Steps>

Locations follow your organization's structure: an archived project or team
cannot become the location of a new space.

## Choose a location

| Location         | Listed in                                | While access is inherited, who can edit |
| ---------------- | ---------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------- |
| **Organization** | The sidebar's **Spaces**, for everyone   | Everyone in the organization            |
| **Project**      | The project's navigation                 | Members of the project                  |
| **Team**         | The project's navigation, under its team | Members of the team                     |

A space owns no delivery configuration: moving work items never changes a
space, and moving a space never changes work items. Pick the location by who
maintains the knowledge: a team's runbooks belong to the team, a product
area's decisions to the project, a company handbook to the organization.

## The spaces list

**Spaces** in the sidebar lists the organization's spaces and those of your
projects and teams; **All spaces** opens every space you can read,
organization spaces first, then by project. A filter appears when the list
grows; **Show archived spaces** reveals archived ones.

![The Spaces page: the organization’s Engineering Handbook first, then the Payments project’s space, each row with its icon, description, page count and last update, and New space](/screenshots/spaces-list.webp)

Spaces you cannot read are not listed, except Restricted spaces you
administer without reading them: they appear by name only under
**Restricted spaces you administer** (see
[administrators](/spaces/access#administrators)).

## A space's home

![The home of a project space: its name and facts (location, publishing policy, your access), New page, the page tree with Find a page, Recently updated, and Linked work with how much of it is done](/screenshots/spaces-space-home.webp)

- **The header** shows the location, the publishing policy (**Direct
  publishing** or **Reviewed publishing**), **Restricted** when it is, and
  your access (**Can view**, **Can comment** or **Can edit**).
- **New page** adds a page at the end of the space (editors only).
- **Space settings** (the sliders button), for editors and administrators.
- **Pull requests**, in Reviewed spaces: open proposals across its pages.
- **Pages**: the tree, with **Find a page**. See [pages](/spaces/pages).
- **Recently updated**: the latest published versions, with who published
  them and their message.
- **Linked work**, in project and team spaces: work items its pages are
  linked to (mentioned by a published version, created from a page,
  attached to an item or cited by a specification). Each appears once with
  how it is linked (_Mentioned, attached · Capture retry design_), open work
  first, with in-progress and done counts. Items you cannot see are left
  out. See [work in your pages](/knowledge/in-pages).

## Space settings

Settings apply as soon as you change them (text fields on leaving or
Enter), with **Saving…**, **Saved** or the refusal reason beside them.

| Group                     | Settings                                            | Who can change them                                          |
| ------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Details**               | Name, icon, address, description                    | Editors of the space                                         |
| **Location & publishing** | Location; publishing policy (Direct or Reviewed)    | [Administrators](/spaces/access#administrators) of the space |
| **Access**                | Access mode (Inherited or Restricted); named access | Administrators of the space                                  |
| **Archive**               | Archive or restore the space                        | Administrators of the space                                  |

Changes that decide who can read or edit ask first:

- **Moving an Inherited space** says where it will be listed and who will be
  able to edit it.
- **Restricting** says the location's members lose access unless named, and
  that you keep edit access.

Switching from Direct to Reviewed is refused while a page has unpublished
changes: publish or discard them first. Switching back to Direct reopens
each page's live draft at its published version.

## Archive a space

**Archive space** hides the space from navigation and freezes its pages:
readable through links and history, but nobody can edit, publish or comment.
Nothing is deleted. Archived spaces appear under **Show archived spaces**;
**Restore space** (on the space or in its settings) brings everything
back.

Archiving a project or a team does not archive its spaces or change who can
read them.

## Tips

- Give each project a space. It then shows, with recently published pages,
  on the project's **Refinement** view, and its home lists linked work.
- Write the description as the sentence that tells someone whether a page
  belongs here.
