---
title: Privacy and telemetry
description: A self-hosted installation sends no telemetry. Data leaves the server only for the services you configure.
---

A self-hosted Lictory sends no telemetry to the Lictory project or anyone
else. zero-cache's anonymous usage telemetry is disabled, and zero-cache has
no route to the internet. Outbound connections happen only for what you
configure:

| Setting                                                          | What leaves the server                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| AI providers (admin console, `LICTORY_AI_*` or `OPENAI_API_KEY`) | Content sent to the language model you configure (analysis, chat). Dictation audio to the speech-to-text provider you configure: live OpenAI dictation (the default with OpenAI) streams from the browser to OpenAI; other providers receive short clips through your server, which keeps nothing. Dictation off: `LICTORY_TRANSCRIPTION_PROVIDER=none`, or Off in the console. See [AI providers](/self-hosting/ai-providers) |
| `POSTHOG_PROJECT_TOKEN` + `POSTHOG_HOST`                         | Product analytics: browser events via your `/relay` route, server events                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| `RESEND_*` or `SMTP_*`                                           | Transactional and notification email                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| `S3_*` or `R2_*`                                                 | Uploaded files                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| A project's Jira connection                                      | Handed-off work items and status reads with that Jira site                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| `GOOGLE_*`, `MICROSOFT_*`, `OIDC_*`                              | Sign-in through those identity providers                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| `ACME_EMAIL`, `BACKUP_*`, alert and ping URLs                    | Certificate issuance, encrypted backups, alerts and monitoring pings                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |

The `lictory` command on the server also reaches out, without sending
anything about your installation: `install`, `upgrade`, `upgrade --check` and
`doctor` ask GitHub's API for the newest release of your channel (anonymous
requests; `GITHUB_TOKEN` raises the rate limit), and releases pull their
images from your registry (`ghcr.io` by default). Without that access,
installing and upgrading cannot find releases, while everything else keeps
working and `doctor` only warns that it could not check for a newer release.

Uploaded documents are converted to text on your server; scanned PDFs are
rejected rather than sent to a hosted OCR service. Lictory Intelligence runs
only when the server has an AI key **and** an organization administrator has
enabled it; turning it off fails queued runs before any content is sent.

Analytics are off unless configured, and then follow the consent mode you
choose ([analytics](/self-hosting/configuration#analytics)).

## Your installation's legal notices

You operate your installation, so its terms and privacy notice are yours.
Lictory ships none and never links to the lictory.com notices. Publish your
own and set `LEGAL_TERMS_URL` and `LEGAL_PRIVACY_URL`
([legal notices](/self-hosting/configuration#legal-notices)); without them
the app shows no acceptance notice at sign-up and no legal links.

This documentation site itself loads no third-party scripts. Like
lictory.com, it counts visits with PostHog through its own `/relay`, without
cookies unless you allow them; it never sends search terms or copied text. Its
[privacy notice](https://lictory.com/privacy) covers both Lictory websites.
