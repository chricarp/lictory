---
title: Teams
description: Split a project's work between the groups doing it, give each team its own backlog and pages, and move work between teams safely.
---

A **team** is a group of people working on one vertical of a project, such
as Checkout or Refunds inside Payments. Teams are light: they own no
workflow, types or settings. The project's workflow, readiness policy and
iterations apply to every team. A team gives its work a home: a team
backlog, a board narrowed to it, its own pages.

Every work item belongs to one project and at most one of its teams.

## When to use it

- Several groups share one project and each wants to see its own work.
- Work arrives in the project and someone has to decide which group takes
  it.
- A group keeps its own runbooks, designs or notes and wants them next to
  its work.

A small project with one group doesn't need teams. The project's board and
backlog are enough.

## Create a team

<Steps>
<Step>

### Open the project's teams

![Project settings, Teams: the Checkout team with its description, two members, links to its board and backlog, and Add team](/screenshots/project-teams.webp)

Project administrators open **Project settings → Teams**.

</Step>
<Step>

### Add the team

Give it a name (for example `Checkout`) and, optionally, a description of
what it works on.

</Step>
<Step>

### Add its members

Use the members button on the team's row to add people of the project.

</Step>
</Steps>

The team then appears under **Teams** in the project's sidebar, your own
teams first.

Team membership grants no capabilities on work items: what you may do with
items still comes from your [disciplines](/organization/disciplines) in the
project. Membership decides who belongs to the team's spaces.

## Team backlog

Open a team from the sidebar to see its backlog. It has three parts:

- **Backlog**: work the team has taken on but not yet planned into a
  sprint or cycle, in the team's rank order. Drag to rank; use the plan
  button on a row to put it into a sprint, or release it back to the
  project (no team).
- **Planned**: the team's work per iteration, with **Plan sprints** to open
  the project backlog narrowed to the team.
- **No team yet**: the project's open work no team has taken. **Take**
  moves an item into this team.

![The Checkout team backlog: its members, an empty Backlog, the Planned section with Sprint 1 and its items, and No team yet with a Take button](/screenshots/delivery-team-backlog.webp)

The header has **Board** (the project board narrowed to the team),
**Context** (the team's and the project's [context pages](/knowledge/context-pages))
and **New item**, which creates an item for the team.

Ranking, taking and planning work need a discipline in the project that can
**Change properties**.

## Move work between teams

Set an item's **Team** on its item page or in its preview, or use **Take**
on a team backlog. Moving an item between teams never changes its
identifier, its status or its iteration: `PAY-142` stays `PAY-142`, in the
same sprint, in the same column. The move is recorded in the item's
activity.

On the project's board and backlog, the **Team** picker narrows the view to
one team, or to **No team**.

## Team spaces and pages

A space can belong to a team: choose the team as the space's **Location**
when you [create it](/spaces/create). Members of the team can edit its
pages by default, and everyone else in the organization can read them,
unless the space is [restricted](/spaces/access).

Keep the pages the team relies on in its **Context** list: they appear on
the team backlog and on the board when it is narrowed to the team.

## What to watch for

- **Archiving keeps the work.** Archiving a team keeps its items and
  history; they show no active team until it is restored.
- **Teams don't change the workflow.** A team cannot have its own statuses,
  types or sprint cadence. If two groups truly need different workflows,
  they belong in different projects.
- **Jira projects too.** In a project that hands off to Jira, a team backlog
  shows work taken on but not yet handed off, and how many items are in the
  delivery tool. See [Deliver in Jira](/integrations/jira).

## Tips

- Triage new work from **No team yet** once a week, so nothing sits
  unclaimed.
- Put the team's runbooks and on-call notes in a team space and link them
  as team context pages.
- Link the team-narrowed board in the team's chat channel: the address
  keeps the filter.
