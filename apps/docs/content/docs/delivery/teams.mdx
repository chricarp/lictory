---
title: Teams
description: Split a project's work between the groups doing it, give each team its own backlog and pages, and move work between teams safely.
---

A **team** is a group of people working on one vertical of a project, such
as Checkout or Refunds inside Payments. Teams own no workflow, types or
settings; the project's workflow, readiness policy and iterations apply to
every team. A team gets a team backlog, a board narrowed to it and its own
pages.

Every work item belongs to one project and at most one of its teams.

## When to use it

- Several groups share one project and each wants to see its own work.
- Someone has to decide which group takes incoming work.
- A group wants its own runbooks, designs or notes next to its work.

A small project with one group doesn't need teams.

## Create a team

<Steps>
<Step>

### Open the project's teams

![Project settings, Teams: the Checkout team with its description, two members, links to its board and backlog, and Add team](/screenshots/project-teams.webp)

Project administrators open **Project settings → Teams**.

</Step>
<Step>

### Add the team

Give it a name (for example `Checkout`) and, optionally, a description.

</Step>
<Step>

### Add its members

Use the members button on the team's row to add people of the project.

</Step>
</Steps>

The team appears under **Teams** in the project's sidebar, your own teams
first.

Team membership grants no capabilities on work items; those come from your
[disciplines](/organization/disciplines). Membership decides who belongs to
the team's spaces.

## Team backlog

Open a team from the sidebar to see its backlog:

- **Backlog**: work the team has taken on but not planned into a sprint or
  cycle, in rank order. Drag to rank; a row's plan button puts it into a
  sprint or releases it back to the project (no team).
- **Planned**: the team's work per iteration, with **Plan sprints** to open
  the project backlog narrowed to the team.
- **No team yet**: the project's open work no team has taken. **Take**
  moves an item into this team.

![The Checkout team backlog: its members, an empty Backlog, the Planned section with Sprint 1 and its items, and No team yet with a Take button](/screenshots/delivery-team-backlog.webp)

The header has **Board** (the project board narrowed to the team),
**Context** (the team's and the project's [context pages](/knowledge/context-pages))
and **New item**.

Ranking, taking and planning need a discipline that can **Change
properties**.

## Move work between teams

Set an item's **Team** on its page or preview, or use **Take** on a team
backlog. Moving never changes identifier, status or iteration: `PAY-142`
stays `PAY-142`, in the same sprint and column. The move is recorded in the
item's activity.

On the project's board and backlog, the **Team** picker narrows to one team
or **No team**.

## Team spaces and pages

A space can belong to a team: choose the team as its **Location** when you
[create it](/spaces/create). Team members can edit its pages by default and
the rest of the organization can read them, unless the space is
[restricted](/spaces/access).

Pages in the team's **Context** list appear on the team backlog and on the
board narrowed to the team.

## What to watch for

- **Archiving keeps the work.** Archiving a team keeps its items and
  history; they show no active team until it is restored.
- **Teams don't change the workflow.** A team cannot have its own statuses,
  types or sprint cadence. Groups that need different workflows belong in
  different projects.
- **Jira projects too.** In a project that hands off to Jira, a team backlog
  shows work taken on but not yet handed off, and how many items are in the
  delivery tool. See [Deliver in Jira](/integrations/jira).

## Tips

- Triage **No team yet** weekly so nothing sits unclaimed.
- Keep runbooks and on-call notes in a team space, linked as team context
  pages.
- Link the team-narrowed board in the team's channel.
