Every sprint and cycle has a **report**. Open it from the backlog:
**Report** on the active iteration, or a finished one under **Completed**.
Completing a sprint also offers **View report**.

![The Sprint 1 report: Committed at start, Completed, Added after start and Removed after start, the burndown chart with its dashed ideal line, and the sprint’s context page under Pages](/screenshots/delivery-iteration-report.webp)

## What the report shows

- **Totals**: **Committed at start**, **Completed**, **Added after start**,
  **Removed after start** and, once the iteration is completed, **Carried
  over**. Each shows points and item count.
- **Burndown**: remaining points per day, once the iteration has started.
  The dashed line is the ideal pace; the upper line is the scope as it
  changed.
- **Velocity**: committed and completed points of up to eight recent
  completed iterations.
- **Pages**: the iteration's [context pages](/knowledge/context-pages), with
  **Add page** and **Create iteration notes**.

## How the numbers are made

Reports are not computed from the items' current state. Each change that
matters (an item added or removed, an estimate changed, an item completed or
reopened) is recorded as it happens, and the burndown replays those records.

Completing an iteration freezes its totals: later edits, re-estimates or
moves don't rewrite the report.

## Points or items

Reports count points when the project uses estimates, and items when it
doesn't (**No estimates** in **Project settings → Planning**). If nothing in
the iteration is estimated yet, the burndown counts items and says so.
Velocity needs estimates.

T-shirt sizes are stored as points (XS 1, S 2, M 3, L 5, XL 8).

## What to watch for

- **Late estimates count as scope change.** Estimating after the sprint
  started raises the scope line.
- **Reopened work comes back.** Reopening a completed item in an active
  iteration adds its points back to what remains.
- **Velocity is the project's.** A backlog narrowed to a team doesn't
  compare its plan with it.
- **Jira projects have no reports here.** Iterations are planned in Jira in
  external mode.

## Tips

- Read **Added after start** at the retrospective: frequent additions
  usually mean work started before it was ready.
- Link the retrospective notes to the iteration. See
  [iteration notes](/delivery/iterations#iteration-notes).
