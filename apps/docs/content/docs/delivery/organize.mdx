title: Organize work
description: Group work with labels and milestones, find any item with All items and its filters, and archive work you no longer need.

Boards and backlogs show what is being worked on now. To find everything
else (finished work, a release's scope, all the bugs with one label) use
labels, milestones and **All items**.

## Labels

![Project settings, Labels & milestones: the labels customer-facing and payments-core with how many items carry each, New label, and the milestone Safe captures beta with its target date and Planned](/screenshots/project-labels.webp)

Labels tag work across types and stages: `payments-api`, `customer-facing`,
`tech-debt`. Each project has its own labels.

- **On an item**, choose **Add label** in its properties. Pick existing
  labels, or type a **Label name**, pick a color and create a new one.
- **In Project settings → Labels & milestones**, rename labels, change their
  color, and archive or restore them.

Project administrators and people whose discipline can **Change properties**
manage labels. Archiving a label keeps every past assignment.

In a project that hands off to Jira, labels are written to the Jira issue on
each hand-off. Labels added in Jira are kept.

## Milestones

A milestone is a target the project works toward, such as a release, with
an optional target date and a status: **Planned**, **Active**,
**Completed** or **Cancelled**.

- **On an item**, set its **Milestone**, or create one with a **Milestone
  name** and **Target date**.
- **In Project settings → Labels & milestones**, change a milestone's name,
  target date and status, see how many items it holds, and archive or
  restore it.

Milestones are not iterations: they don't burn down and don't have an
active one. Use them to group work across sprints toward one goal.

## All items

**All items**, under **Browse** in the project's sidebar, is the project's
catalog: every proposal, specification and delivery item, including
completed and canceled work.

![All items in Payments grouped by status (Backlog, Unstarted, Started, Done), the type filters above the list, and each row with labels, milestone, pages, readiness checks, owner and last update](/screenshots/delivery-all-items.webp)

- **Types**: the row of types at the top narrows the list to one type, or
  **All types**.
- **Search**: filter by title or identifier.
- **Filters**: **Status**, **Refinement**, **Type**, **Team**, **Priority**,
  **Assignee**, **Parent**, **Labels** and **Milestone**. Each applied filter
  shows as a chip you can remove.
- **Sort and group**: sort by **Last updated**, **Created**, **Priority**,
  **Status** or **Identifier**; group by status; **Show archived** includes
  archived items.

Move through the list with `j` and `k` (or the arrow keys) and open the
selected item with `Enter` or `o`.

### Keep a view

Filters, sort, grouping and **Show archived** live in the page's address.
Bookmark it, or share the link, and it opens the same view for anyone who
can see the project.

### Built-in views

The project's sidebar also has **Views**: **Needs review** (an outstanding
review request), **Blocked** (an open blocking discussion) and **Recently
updated** (changed in the last two days). You cannot create your own named
views; a bookmarked **All items** address serves the same purpose.

## Archive and restore

Archive work that should leave the lists without being deleted: a
duplicate, an idea the team dropped, an obsolete Story.

<Steps>
<Step>

### Archive

On the item page, open **More actions** (**⋯**) and choose **Archive**.

</Step>
<Step>

### Find it again

Turn on **Show archived** in **All items**.

</Step>
<Step>

### Restore

Open the archived item and choose **More actions → Restore**.

</Step>
</Steps>

Archiving needs a discipline that can **Change properties**. Nothing is
deleted: the specification, revisions, discussions, decisions and activity
stay.

### What to watch for

- **Archiving a parent archives its children.** Its active descendants are
  archived with it. Restoring the parent does not restore them, so no child
  comes back unseen: restore each one you still need.
- **Archived work leaves product knowledge.** Archived items, and the
  decisions recorded on them, no longer appear in product knowledge search
  until they are restored. Done and canceled items are not archived and stay
  searchable. See [product knowledge](/search/product-knowledge).
- **Cancel instead of archiving finished decisions.** If the team decided
  not to build something, a canceled status keeps the item visible as
  history; archiving hides it.

## Tips

- Use labels for cross-cutting themes and milestones for dated goals. Don't
  encode team or type in labels: teams and types already filter the list.
- Bookmark the **All items** views you check every week, such as open Bugs
  of your team.
- Mark a duplicate with a **Duplicates** [relation](/delivery/relations)
  before archiving it, so readers find the original.
