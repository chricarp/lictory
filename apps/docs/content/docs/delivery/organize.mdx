title: Organize work
description: Group work with labels and milestones, find any item with All items and its filters, and archive work you no longer need.
---

Boards and backlogs show current work. For everything else (finished work,
a release's scope, every bug with one label) use labels, milestones and
**All items**.

## Labels

![Project settings, Labels & milestones: the labels customer-facing and payments-core with how many items carry each, New label, and the milestone Safe captures beta with its target date and Planned](/screenshots/project-labels.webp)

Labels tag work across types and stages (`payments-api`, `customer-facing`,
`tech-debt`). Each project has its own.

- **On an item**, choose **Add label** in its properties: pick existing
  labels, or type a **Label name** and pick a color to create one.
- **In Project settings → Labels & milestones**, rename, recolor, archive or
  restore labels.

Project administrators and people whose discipline can **Change properties**
manage labels. Archiving a label keeps every past assignment.

In a Jira project, labels are written to the Jira issue on each hand-off;
labels added in Jira are kept.

## Milestones

A milestone is a target such as a release, with an optional target date and
a status: **Planned**, **Active**, **Completed** or **Cancelled**.

- **On an item**, set its **Milestone**, or create one with a **Milestone
  name** and **Target date**.
- **In Project settings → Labels & milestones**, change its name, target
  date and status, see its item count, and archive or restore it.

Milestones are not iterations: they don't burn down and none is active. They
group work across sprints toward one goal.

## All items

**All items**, under **Browse** in the project's sidebar, lists every
proposal, specification and delivery item, including completed and canceled
work.

![All items in Payments grouped by status (Backlog, Unstarted, Started, Done), the type filters above the list, and each row with labels, milestone, pages, readiness checks, owner and last update](/screenshots/delivery-all-items.webp)

- **Types**: one type, or **All types**.
- **Search**: title or identifier.
- **Filters**: **Status**, **Refinement**, **Type**, **Team**, **Priority**,
  **Assignee**, **Parent**, **Labels** and **Milestone**, each shown as a
  removable chip.
- **Sort and group**: sort by **Last updated**, **Created**, **Priority**,
  **Status** or **Identifier**; group by status; **Show archived** includes
  archived items.

`j` and `k` (or the arrow keys) move through the list; `Enter` or `o` opens
the selected item.

### Keep a view

Filters, sort, grouping and **Show archived** live in the address, so a
bookmark or shared link opens the same view for anyone who can see the
project.

### Built-in views

The project's sidebar also has **Views**: **Needs review** (an outstanding
review request), **Blocked** (an open blocking discussion) and **Recently
updated** (changed in the last two days). There are no custom named views;
bookmark an **All items** address instead.

## Archive and restore

Archive work that should leave the lists without being deleted, such as a
duplicate or an obsolete Story.

<Steps>
<Step>

### Archive

On the item page, open **More actions** (**⋯**) and choose **Archive**.

</Step>
<Step>

### Find it again

Turn on **Show archived** in **All items**.

</Step>
<Step>

### Restore

Open the archived item and choose **More actions → Restore**.

</Step>
</Steps>

Archiving needs a discipline that can **Change properties**. Nothing is
deleted: the specification, revisions, discussions, decisions and activity
stay.

### What to watch for

- **Archiving a parent archives its children.** Restoring the parent does
  not restore them: restore each one you still need.
- **Archived work leaves product knowledge.** Archived items and their
  decisions disappear from product knowledge search until restored. Done and
  canceled items stay searchable. See
  [product knowledge](/search/product-knowledge).
- **Cancel instead of archiving finished decisions.** If the team decided
  not to build something, a canceled status keeps the item visible as
  history; archiving hides it.

## Tips

- Use labels for cross-cutting themes and milestones for dated goals; don't
  encode team or type in labels.
- Bookmark the **All items** views you check weekly, such as your team's open
  Bugs.
- Mark a duplicate with a **Duplicates** [relation](/delivery/relations)
  before archiving it, so readers find the original.
