The **Board** shows a native project's work as cards in columns, one column
per workflow status. Drag a card to change its status; drag it within a
column to change its rank. Open it from the sidebar: **Board**, under the
project's delivery section.

## What the board shows

- **Columns** are the project's workflow statuses in order. Each status
  belongs to a fixed category (backlog, unstarted, started, done, canceled),
  which gives it meaning for readiness, reports and progress. The
  **Canceled** column is hidden until you turn on **Show canceled column**.
  The default workflow is **Backlog**, **Todo**, **In Progress**, **In
  Review**, **Done** and **Canceled**; project administrators change it in
  **Project settings → Workflow & types** (the gear on the board opens it).
- **Cards** show the identifier, title, assignee, priority, estimate and
  team. Spec-backed items also show their refinement (`Spec: ready`, or
  `Spec: in review · 3/5 checks`), open blockers and, for parents, how many
  children are done.
- **Implementation work only.** Epics and other higher-level items are not
  cards: their children are. See [Epics](/delivery#epics).
- **Recent closed work.** Done and canceled items leave the board two weeks
  after their last change when you view all work. They stay in
  [All items](/delivery/organize#all-items) and in iteration reports.

## Move work

<Steps>
<Step>

### Drag a card

Drop it in another column to change its status, or between two cards to
rank it. You need a discipline that can **Change properties** in the
project.

</Step>
<Step>

### Respect the readiness policy

Moving a spec-backed item into a started status follows the project's
[readiness policy](/delivery#readiness-policy). With **Warn**, the move goes
through and the board tells you the item started before its specification
was ready. With **Block**, the board refuses the move and names the failing
checks.

</Step>
</Steps>

The first move into a started status also records the item's current
revision as [accepted for delivery](/delivery#accepting-automatically), if
no one accepted it yet.

To create an item in a column, use the **+** in the column's header. New
items go into the sprint you are viewing and the team you narrowed to.

## Narrow the board

The controls in the header combine, and live in the address, so a link
shares the same view:

- **Sprint** or **Cycle**: the active iteration (the default when the
  project uses iterations), a planned one, or **All work**.
- **Team**: **All teams**, one team, or **No team**. See
  [teams](/delivery/teams).
- **Assignee**: **Anyone**, chosen people, or **Unassigned**.
- **Types**: **All types** or chosen work item types.
- **Board options**: swimlanes by **Assignee**, **Parent** or **Priority**
  (or **No swimlanes**), and **Show canceled column**. Dragging between
  swimlanes also changes the assignee or priority.

When the project uses sprints and none is active, the board says so and
offers **Plan in the backlog**.

## Preview an item

Click a card to preview it beside the board without leaving it. See
[preview an item](/delivery/relations#preview-an-item). Hold `Ctrl` or `⌘`
while clicking to open the item in a new tab instead.

## Context pages

**Context** in the header lists the pages the work relies on: the
project's, the narrowed team's and the viewed sprint's, each in its own
list. Project administrators and people who can **Change properties** can
**Add page**, reorder and remove them, and create
[iteration notes](/delivery/iterations#iteration-notes). You only see pages
you can read.

![The Context popover on the Payments board: the project's pages in one list, Sprint 1's pages in another, with Add page and Create iteration notes](/screenshots/detail-context-pages.webp)

See [context pages](/knowledge/context-pages) for what to keep there.

## What to watch for

- **Jira projects have no board.** In a project that hands off to Jira,
  **Board** is replaced by **Hand-off**. See
  [Deliver in Jira](/integrations/jira).
- **No transition rules.** Any status can move to any other. Categories
  carry the meaning; there are no rules to configure.
- **Viewers can't drag.** Without **Change properties**, cards open but
  don't move.

## Tips

- Narrow the board to your team and link it in the team's channel: the
  address keeps the filters.
- Use swimlanes by **Parent** during a sprint to see how each Story's tasks
  are progressing.
- Keep the sprint's goal and the runbooks the work depends on in
  **Context**, so nobody has to search for them during standup.
