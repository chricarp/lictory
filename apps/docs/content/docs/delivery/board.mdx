The **Board** shows a native project's work as cards, one column per
workflow status. Open it from **Board** in the project's sidebar.

## What the board shows

- **Columns** are the workflow statuses in order. Each belongs to a fixed
  category (backlog, unstarted, started, done, canceled) that readiness,
  reports and progress rely on. **Canceled** is hidden until you turn on
  **Show canceled column**. The default workflow is **Backlog**, **Todo**,
  **In Progress**, **In Review**, **Done** and **Canceled**; project
  administrators change it in **Project settings → Workflow & types** (the
  gear on the board).
- **Cards** show identifier, title, assignee, priority, estimate and team.
  Spec-backed items add their refinement (`Spec: ready`, or
  `Spec: in review · 3/5 checks`), open blockers and, for parents, how many
  children are done.
- **Implementation work only.** Epics and other higher-level items are not
  cards: their children are. See [Epics](/delivery#epics).
- **Recent closed work.** In **All work**, done and canceled items leave the
  board two weeks after their last change. They stay in
  [All items](/delivery/organize#all-items) and iteration reports.

## Move work

<Steps>
<Step>

### Drag a card

Drop it in another column to change its status, or between two cards to
rank it. You need a discipline that can **Change properties**.

</Step>
<Step>

### Respect the readiness policy

Moving a spec-backed item into a started status follows the
[readiness policy](/delivery#readiness-policy). With **Warn**, the move goes
through and the board notes the item started before it was ready. With
**Block**, the board refuses and names the failing checks.

</Step>
</Steps>

The first move into a started status also records the current revision as
[accepted for delivery](/delivery#accepting-automatically) if no one accepted
it yet.

The **+** in a column header creates an item there, in the sprint and team
you are viewing.

## Narrow the board

The header controls combine and live in the address, so a link shares the
view:

- **Sprint** or **Cycle**: the active iteration (the default when the
  project uses iterations), a planned one, or **All work**.
- **Team**: **All teams**, one team, or **No team**. See
  [teams](/delivery/teams).
- **Assignee**: **Anyone**, chosen people, or **Unassigned**.
- **Types**: **All types** or chosen work item types.
- **Board options**: swimlanes by **Assignee**, **Parent** or **Priority**
  (or **No swimlanes**), and **Show canceled column**. Dragging between
  swimlanes also changes the assignee or priority.

With sprints and none active, the board offers **Plan in the backlog**.

## Preview an item

Click a card to [preview it](/delivery/relations#preview-an-item) beside the
board. `Ctrl`- or `⌘`-click opens it in a new tab.

## Context pages

**Context** in the header lists the pages the work relies on: the
project's, the narrowed team's and the viewed sprint's. Project
administrators and people who can **Change properties** can **Add page**,
reorder and remove them, and create
[iteration notes](/delivery/iterations#iteration-notes). You only see pages
you can read.

![The Context popover on the Payments board: the project's pages in one list, Sprint 1's pages in another, with Add page and Create iteration notes](/screenshots/detail-context-pages.webp)

See [context pages](/knowledge/context-pages) for what to keep there.

## What to watch for

- **Jira projects have no board.** In a project that hands off to Jira,
  **Board** is replaced by **Hand-off**. See
  [Deliver in Jira](/integrations/jira).
- **No transition rules.** Any status can move to any other.
- **Viewers can't drag.** Without **Change properties**, cards open but
  don't move.

## Tips

- Narrow the board to your team and link it in the team's channel.
- Swimlanes by **Parent** show how each Story's tasks are progressing.
- Keep the sprint goal and runbooks in **Context** so nobody searches for
  them during standup.
