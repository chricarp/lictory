---
title: From proposal to ready
description: How a spec-backed work item moves from a rough proposal through refinement and review to ready, and where each part of that work happens in Lictory.
---

Refinement turns "Product wants to build this" into "Engineering can safely
commit to building this", on one **work item**: the same `PAY-2` starts as a
rough proposal, collects revisions, discussions and reviews, becomes ready,
and is delivered in Lictory or handed off to a delivery tool.

<Lifecycle />

The path is not a straight line. A specification can go back from review to
refinement, a ready one can stop being ready when its text changes, and
delivered work can be amended by a later revision. Every step stays in the
item's history.

## Which work items have a specification

Every work item has a **type** that either carries a **specification** or is
plain work:

| Default type | Carries a specification | What that means                                         |
| ------------ | ----------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| Epic, Story  | Yes                     | Refined, reviewed, approved and checked for readiness   |
| Task, Bug    | No                      | A description edited directly, then planned and tracked |
| Subtask      | No                      | Plain work under another item                           |

Organization administrators add and change types; each project enables the
ones it uses. The create dialog's type picker marks types with a
specification as **Specification**.

Only spec-backed items go through refinement. A Task keeps a history of its
description but has no reviews, approvals or readiness checks of its own;
comments on it are a conversation under the description.

## The stages

The card under a spec-backed item's title names its stage in one sentence,
shows the evidence and says what happens **Next**. The stages are the
refinement states, named as in the **Refinement** property and the Refinement
view's tabs, then delivery: **Draft → In refinement → In review → Ready →
Delivery**.

| Refinement state | The card says                                                   | What moves it on                                                                                             |
| ---------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Draft            | **Shape the proposal**                                          | **Request reviews** when the intent is clear; **Start refinement** first to explore it before anyone decides |
| In refinement    | **Agree on what to build**                                      | Requesting a review moves the item to _In review_                                                            |
| In review        | **Review the current specification**                            | Reviewers approve the current revision; blocking discussions are resolved                                    |
| Ready            | **Ready for delivery**                                          | Every [readiness check](/review/readiness) passes, so Lictory sets it for you                                |
| Delivery         | **Accepted for delivery**, **Delivery underway**, **Delivered** | Accepting the revision (in Lictory) or handing it off (to Jira)                                              |

![PAY-2 "Retry failed card captures" in review: revision 2 of the specification with its review rail, required Engineering and QA reviews and readiness checks beside the text](/screenshots/specification-review.webp)

The card's **Spec** row summarizes the current revision: readiness checks
passing (for example _3/5 checks_), required reviews approved and blocking
discussions. Each summary opens its section: **Readiness**, **Review** in the
side panel, **Discussions**. Once a revision is accepted for delivery, a
**Delivery** row adds the workflow status and the accepted and delivered
revisions.

## Refinement state and delivery status

Two separate facts describe a spec-backed item, never merged into one
field:

- **Refinement state** says how far the specification has got: **Draft**,
  **In refinement**, **In review** or **Ready**.
- **Delivery status** says how far the work has got: the project's workflow
  status (Backlog, Todo, In Progress, Done…) in a native project, or the
  delivery tool's status in an external one.

A specification can be **Ready** while its work has not started, and an
item can be **In Progress** while a later revision is back **In review**. The
item shows both side by side.

**Ready** is evidence, not a choice: it is set when every readiness check
passes for the current revision and removed (back to **In review**) when one
stops passing. People with the _change properties_ capability can move an
item between **Draft**, **In refinement** and **In review** in the
**Refinement** property, but not to or from **Ready** against the checks.

## Protected specifications

A specification's agreed text is its current revision. While it is taking
shape, people whose discipline may _edit specifications_ change it directly:
they write together in the editor and save revisions. See
[live drafts](/specifications/live-drafts).

The first time the item becomes **Ready**, the specification is
**protected**, and stays protected even if the item later leaves Ready.
Nobody edits it directly any more: changes are **proposed**, reviewed and
applied as the next revision, and a proposed change can also carry the item's
breakdown into child work. See [proposed changes](/specifications/branches).

<Callout title="Propose a change from the text">
  Select a passage of a specification and choose **Suggest a change**: write the
  new wording beside the text, and the person asked accepts it as the next
  revision (on a ready specification, its reviewers approve it). Larger changes
  open a branch at that passage instead. See [suggest
  changes](/specifications/suggestions).
</Callout>

## The project's views

The project sidebar groups refinement under **Refine**:

- **Proposals**: spec-backed items in **Draft** that have not started
  refinement, ideas not yet worth the team's time. **New proposal** creates
  one.
- **Refinement**: specifications being clarified, reviewed or ready, not yet
  in delivery, in tabs by refinement state (**All**, **In refinement**, **In
  review**, **Ready**). An item already in delivery comes back here only when
  its specification changes (an _amendment_); delivery carries on with the
  accepted revision meanwhile. Above the list, the project's knowledge strip
  shows its spaces and recently published pages.
- **My reviews** opens **Needs my review**: reviews asked of you or your
  disciplines on the current revision, and suggestions waiting for your
  approval, each with the revision and the number of open blocking
  discussions.

**All items**, under **Browse**, is the catalog of every item of every type
and stage, including done, canceled and (when you ask) archived work. See
[organizing work](/delivery/organize).

![The Refinement view of the Payments project: the refinement-state tabs with their counts, the Payments knowledge strip, and each item with its next step](/screenshots/work-refinement.webp)

Refinement is the project's home page when the project has spec-backed
types. An item opened from one of these views keeps the view highlighted in
the sidebar and first in the breadcrumb, one click back to the list.

**Ready for delivery** means the checks pass, not a commitment: the team
still decides when to build it, by accepting the revision in a native project
or handing it off in an external one. See [delivery](/delivery) and
[Jira](/integrations/jira).
