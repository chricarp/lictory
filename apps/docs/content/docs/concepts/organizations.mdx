```text
Organization   a company, or a very large department of one
└─ Project     a product area: identifiers, workflow, delivery and its people
   └─ Team     a vertical inside the product: a group of people and a label
```

| Scope        | Owns                                                                                                                                                                        |
| ------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Organization | Name and branding, members and invitations, application roles, work item types, Jira connections, whether Lictory Intelligence may run, projects                            |
| Project      | Key and numbering, members and project roles, disciplines, teams, workflow, enabled types, labels and milestones, iterations, estimates, delivery mode, Definition of Ready |
| Team         | Name, description, members                                                                                                                                                  |

A work item belongs to exactly one project (its identity, `PAY-142`) and
optionally one of the project's teams. Moving it between teams never changes
its identifier, status or iteration. A team owns no configuration: boards and
backlogs can be narrowed to it, and team membership grants nothing.

## Roles and disciplines

Three separate questions, three separate concepts:

- **Application role**: _can this person administer the organization?_
  Owners and admins manage the organization and every project; everyone else
  is a member.
- **Project role**: _can this person configure the project?_ Project admins
  manage its settings; members work in it.
- **Discipline**: _what kind of work does this person do in this project, and
  what may they do with its work items?_ Product, Engineering and QA exist in
  every new project; administrators can add others (Security, UX…).

Roles grant no work item capabilities. Those come from disciplines, and a
person's capabilities in a project are the union of the disciplines they
hold there:

| Capability             | Allows                                                                    |
| ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Edit specifications    | Create spec-backed items, revise main, restore revisions, merge into main |
| Change properties      | Status, priority, owner, parent, labels, iterations, ordering, archiving  |
| Request reviews        | Request and cancel reviews                                                |
| Can be asked to review | Be a review responsibility and decide reviews assigned to the discipline  |
| Hand off               | Export ready work to the project's Jira project and link existing issues  |

New projects start with Product (every capability), Engineering (request
reviews, be asked to review, change properties) and QA (request reviews, be
asked to review). Taking part in discussions only needs organization
membership. Every check runs on the server; the interface only hides
controls you cannot use.

Every organization member can read every project. There are no private
projects.

## Deleting an organization

An owner deletes an organization from **Organization settings → Delete
organization**. Export its data first: the page shows the latest export and
links to **Data export**. Type the organization's handle to confirm.

Deletion is not immediate. Scheduling it locks the organization at once:
nobody can open its projects or change anything in it, and every member is
told by email. Owners can still download exports and **Cancel deletion**.
After the grace period (30 days unless your installation sets another) its
projects, work items, specifications with every revision, reviews,
approvals, discussions, decisions, attachments, Jira connections with their
credentials, and its audit log are permanently deleted. People's accounts and
their other organizations are not affected. The installation's history keeps
a record that the organization existed and was deleted.
