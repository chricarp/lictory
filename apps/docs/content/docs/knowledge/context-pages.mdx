title: Context pages
description: Keep the pages a project, team or iteration relies on next to the board, backlog and iteration report, and create iteration notes.
---

A **context page** is a page a delivery surface keeps at hand. Each project,
team and iteration has a short, ordered list of them. Linking or removing one
never moves work.

![The Context popover on the board: the project's pages and Sprint 1's pages, each with its space, Add page and Create iteration notes](/screenshots/detail-context-pages.webp)

## When to use it

- **For an iteration**: its goal and notes during the sprint, its
  retrospective after it.
- **For a team**: the team's working agreement, the runbooks of what it
  operates.
- **For the project**: the requirements, designs and decision records most
  of its work refers to.

For a page that belongs to one work item, [attach it](/knowledge/in-work#attach-a-page)
to the item instead.

## Where context pages show

The **Context** control (a book icon with a count) in each delivery surface's
header lists the pages in view:

| Surface                       | Lists shown                                                     |
| ----------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Board**                     | The project's, the filtered team's and the selected iteration's |
| **Backlog**                   | The project's and the filtered team's                           |
| Each iteration in the backlog | That iteration's, as a compact icon with its count              |
| A team's backlog              | The team's and the project's                                    |
| The iteration report          | The iteration's, listed under **Pages**                         |

You see and count only pages you can read. An iteration's compact control is
hidden while it has no pages and you cannot add any.

## Add a context page

<Steps>
<Step>

**Open the list.** Choose **Context** on the board, backlog or team backlog,
or the iteration's icon, or go to the iteration report's **Pages**.

</Step>
<Step>

**Add page.** Under the list you want (the project, the team or the
iteration), choose **Add page** and search by title or space (_Add a page to
Sprint 1_).

</Step>
<Step>

**Pick the page.** It is added at the end.

</Step>
</Steps>

### Order and remove

Each page has buttons to move it up or down (_Move "…" up_) and to remove it
(_Remove "…" from Sprint 1_). Removing offers **Undo**; the page's activity
keeps who linked and removed it.

## Create iteration notes

<Steps>
<Step>

**Create iteration notes.** In the iteration's list (the board's or the
iteration's **Context**, or the iteration report's **Pages**), choose
**Create iteration notes**.

</Step>
<Step>

**Choose where.** Pick the **Space** (spaces you can edit, the project's own
first) and the **Title** (_Sprint 1 notes_).

</Step>
<Step>

**Create and open.** The page is created from the
[iteration notes template](/spaces/templates) with the goal, a live list of
the iteration's work, risks and a retrospective, linked to the iteration and
opened.

</Step>
</Steps>

![Sprint 1 notes in the Payments space: the Goal, a live work list of the sprint’s items with their status, then Risks, Notes and the Retrospective headings to fill in](/screenshots/delivery-iteration-notes.webp)

Creating the page publishes its first version. Creating and linking happen
together: if the link is refused, no page is created, and retrying after a
lost connection never creates two. Work never changes the notes afterwards.

If you can edit no space yet, ask for edit access to a project space, or
create one.

## Who can manage context pages

Project administrators and members whose discipline can change item
properties, also on completed iterations (for retrospectives). Adding a page
needs read access to it. Everyone else sees the lists without the controls.

## In external projects

A project that delivers in Jira or another tool has no iterations, so no
iteration context. Its team backlogs still show **Context** with the team's
and the project's lists, and every other [knowledge link](/knowledge) works
the same.

## What to watch for

- **Readable pages only.** Two people can see different lists and counts on
  the same board.
- **Archived pages leave the list** until they are restored, and pages of an
  archived space with them.
- **A team or an iteration, not both.** Each list belongs to the project,
  one team or one iteration.
- **Teams must be active**: an archived team takes no new context pages.

## Tips

- Create a sprint's notes when you start it, and keep the retrospective
  there.
- Keep each list short. Three well-chosen pages are read; ten are not.
- Link the project's main requirements page and design doc to the project.
