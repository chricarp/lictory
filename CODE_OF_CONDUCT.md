# Community code of conduct

Treat people with respect in issues, pull requests, discussions and other
project spaces. Welcome contributors with different backgrounds and experience.
Critique ideas and behavior with evidence; make feedback specific and actionable.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, sexual attention, doxxing,
impersonation and publishing private information are unacceptable. Do not share
customer data, credentials or another person's private correspondence.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, warn participants, temporarily
restrict participation or ban repeated or serious offenders. Explain the reason
privately where appropriate and protect reporters' identities. A maintainer
involved in a report should not decide its resolution alone where another
maintainer is available.

Report concerns privately to the repository owner using their GitHub profile's
contact method. Do not disclose sensitive reports in public issues. A dedicated
monitored community contact must be published before the public launch; see
[the launch checklist](docs/operations/open-source-release.md). To ask for
reconsideration, contact an uninvolved maintainer where available and describe
the decision and relevant evidence.
