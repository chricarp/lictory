# Code of conduct

Everyone participating in Lictory issues, pull requests, reviews and other
project spaces should be able to contribute without harassment.

Be respectful, give actionable feedback, acknowledge mistakes, and discuss
ideas and evidence. Respect privacy and different backgrounds, abilities and
levels of experience. Disagreement about product or technical choices is welcome.

Harassment, discrimination, sexualized attention, threats, personal attacks,
doxxing, impersonation, and publishing private information without consent are
not acceptable. Do not retaliate against someone who reports a concern.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report concerns privately to [me@chricarp.com](mailto:me@chricarp.com). Do not
put private conduct reports in public issues or use the security advisory
channel for conduct reports.

The current enforcement owner is Christian Carpinelli (@chricarp). A report
should describe the behavior and context, with links where safe. Reports will
be handled privately, with information shared only as needed to investigate and
respond; absolute confidentiality cannot be promised.

Lictory currently has one maintainer. Reports involving that maintainer can use
the same private address, but there is no independent reviewer or appeal contact
at present. This limitation is explicit. When another maintainer or a trusted
independent contact joins, publish their agreed reporting address and require
any maintainer involved in a complaint to recuse themselves.

Maintainers may remove harmful content, give a warning, limit participation or
ban someone according to severity and repetition. Explain the reason privately
where safe, allow a response, and consider an appeal with new evidence. Threats
or disclosure of private information may require immediate removal. Apply these
standards consistently regardless of someone's role or contribution history.

This is Lictory's project policy, not a claim of adoption of another project's
standard covenant.
